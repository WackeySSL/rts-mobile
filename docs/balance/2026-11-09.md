Source: GitHub Actions run [42639401142](https://github.com/WackeySSL/rts-mobile/actions/runs/42639401142)
("Balance run", suite `balance`) on main `b6bfe95`, impl `native`, 190 matches, at most 13500 ticks

there is no previous report to compare against. Seeds are 1..N of each set; the held-out gate seeds

## 1. Main finding: the seat decides every set

The top seat wins almost every equal AI-vs-AI match:

| Set | Pairing, scenario | N | Top seat win share (per-mille) | Bottom seat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| tuning | normal,normal, cross | 100 | 980 | 20 |
| fossil-mirror | normal,normal, fossil-mirror | 20 | 1000 | 0 |
| renew-mirror | normal,normal, renew-mirror | 20 | 1000 | 0 |
| ladder-easy | normal,easy, cross | 20 | 950 | 50 |
| ladder-hard | hard,normal, cross | 20 | 975 | 25 |
| baseline | dummy,easy, cross | 10 | 500 | 500 |

(A draw or a timeout counts half to each side.) The mirrors are the clean test: same faction, same
level, same units, and the bottom seat wins 0 of 40. In the 100-match tuning set the faction split is
480/520, so faction is not what decides; the seat is (980/20).

Only the baseline (dummy vs easy) is seat-balanced (500/500), because the dummy never wins from
either seat. That points at something that acts only when two thinking AIs meet, not at the map alone; P195 settles it.

What this means for reading the rest of the report: faction win shares, level win shares (easy 450 vs
normal 550 in ladder-easy, hard 525 vs normal 475 in ladder-hard) and the kills-per-1000-credits and
survival columns cannot be read as unit or level strength while one seat wins about 98% of matches.
The level shares in the two ladders are the seat share seen through which level sat at the top:
the ladders run both level orders (`easy,normal` and `normal,easy`; `hard,normal` and `normal,hard`)
and each lands near 50% per level only because the seat flips between the two shards. Kills and
survival mix the winner's and the loser's rows. They are listed in section 3 only so the next report
has a before-value.

Row P195 (running now) diagnoses the cause of the seat bias. Tuning rows (P192) wait for its fix.

## 5. Seat-independent findings

The seat bias distorts who wins and so which rows die and are rebuilt. Findings below hold in every
set, including the baseline where seats are balanced, and so are not explained by the seat.

- Production mix. The share per type is nearly identical in tuning (576/207/103/63/35/17), fossil-mirror
  (579/209/97/62/36/18) and renew-mirror (572/200/109/77/27/15). Normal's tier-1 role weights in
  `data/ai/opponent_*.tres` are 35 (anti-infantry), 30 (anti-vehicle), 25 (tank), 10 (artillery), yet
  rifle squads are about 57% of units made and tanks about 6-7%. The realised mix is far from the
  weights in all sets, so the cause is in how a role is chosen or paid for (cheap units are queued
  first or most often), not in who wins. P195 or a later AI row should look at the draw; a weight
  change alone is not a justified proposal yet.
- Harvesters, tankers and scouts sit at 1.5-3.6% of production in every Normal set. They are produced
  to replace losses and on a timer (scout every `scout_interval_ticks` 2700), so a share this low is
  mostly by design; the flag band suits combat units, not these three. This is a threshold question
  for the report format, not a tuning one.
- Match length. Even the seat-balanced baseline runs 10.8 min median (9693-11510 ticks), where easy
  fights a dummy that never defends. Normal vs normal medians (11779-12468 ticks) end soon after
  Normal's `all_in_tick` (10800): the matches seem to be decided by the all-in, not by the waves.
  This is a reading of the medians, not a measurement of when each match's last building fell.
- Every type is used. In the tuning, mirror and ladder sets each unit type of each faction was produced
  (smallest: ladder-easy light_tank 5, renew_scout 13). The baseline's zeros are by design.

## 7. Phase 4 gate lines this run already fails

The gate box stays unticked. This run (seeds 1-100, not the held-out 1001-1100) shows:

- "Normal wins 40-60% against the mirror AI over 100 matches": fails. The bottom seat wins 20 per-mille
  of the 100 tuning matches and the top seat 980; whichever side the player takes, it wins or loses
  with the seat. Faction shares (480/520) are in band but do not measure this.
- "No match lasts over 15 minutes": fails. 4 tuning matches reached the 13500-tick cap, as did 2 in
  ladder-easy and 1 in ladder-hard; the cap is exactly 15.0 min. The median normal-vs-normal match is
  13.3 min.
- "Every unit type is used": met in the tuning set (all 12 types produced, the lowest 15 per-mille),
  but the rifle squads are 57% of production, so the types are used unevenly. The run does not test
  this line on the held-out seeds.

## 8. Proposals

At most three, each one number in `data/`, only for findings the seat cannot explain. All are
after P195: no number should change while one seat wins about 98% of matches, since the next run
could not show its effect.

1. After P195 (hypothesis, to be tested one number at a time): `data/ai/difficulty/normal.tres`
   `all_in_tick` 10800 -> 7200. Evidence: Normal vs normal medians end about 1000-1700 ticks after 10800
   (section 5), and the median is 13.3 min against the 8-10 min target. Expected effect: median length
   moves toward 9-11 min and the timeouts at 13500 ticks drop. Risk: it also moves Normal's
   behaviour against Easy and Hard; compare the ladders on the same seeds.

No second or third proposal. The production mix (rifle 57%) is the biggest seat-independent finding
but its cause is not a data number: the role weights (35/30/25/10) do not match the realised mix, so
changing one weight would be a guess. The harvester, tanker and scout shares are by design (section 5).
