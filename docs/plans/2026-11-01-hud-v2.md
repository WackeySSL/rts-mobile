demand and folds away on a map touch, the same parts in landscape and portrait (only the drawer's
side changes), our names, our rules (ADR 0025 §15); and structures placed first and built on the
spot (ADR 0026).

## Test plan (what is proven headless)
gdUnit4 at the six sizes of `tests/support/screen_sizes.gd`, the portrait profiles, the short
landscape profiles (`web_phone_small` 800 x 304 dp, `web_phone` 914 x 355 dp) and a new profile
with the design's insets (`ui_profiles.notch_design`: landscape 59 sides / 21 bottom, portrait 59
top / 34 bottom). No timing run; the perf suites only inside `verify.sh`.
- **Layout (P119 on):** `HudCorners.place` as a pure function on crafted inputs: every rect inside
  the safe rect (the screen less 12 dp a side), no two overlapping, every tappable rect at least
  48 dp a side (BUILD 64, the strike button 72, groups 56, orders 64 high), mirrored and not, with
  and without the command bar, the Ready chip, the strike button, the full map, the toast and the
  open drawer. ADR 0025 §2's table of numbers is pinned row by row: the right column, the full
  map's two places (under the scope, or left of the column when the strike button leaves no room),
  the group column's room and items per column on `web_phone_small` (152 dp with the bar: two per
  column), `web_phone` and the 6-inch phone, and the 40 % line at `web_phone_portrait` (360 x 700:
  the bar and the column within 270 dp, the column wrapped into two columns of three). In the
  scene: the nodes take those rects, at each size and after a turn.
- **The freeze (P122):** during placement and during the strike aim a group tap that fills or
  empties the selection moves neither the bar's slot, nor BUILD, nor the strike button and its
  check mark and X (rect equality across the tap); the layout follows the selection again when the
  mode ends.
- **Unfold rules (P119c, P120, P121, P119b, P124):** opening one unfold closes the other; a tap, a
  double-tap, a long-press, a pan and a pinch on the map close the drawer, the unpinned full map
  and the economy card and still do their work (a `MOVE` queued, a selection made); a touch on a
  HUD control closes nothing but the economy card; the pinned full map stays; `radar_close_ms`
  with stepped frame time closes it, a touch on it restarts the count; a swipe past
  `drawer_swipe_dp` closes the drawer (landscape: horizontal anywhere on it; portrait: from the
  head only, a vertical drag on the grid scrolls); pause takes every touch; a turn keeps the state;
  a new match starts with nothing unfolded.
- **BUILD (P120, P120c):** from crafted snapshots: the ring's progress is the first-finishing
  head's, the badge the item count, "stalled" (grey, "!") when no head advanced between two
  snapshots with a queue not empty, not on low power and not for a ready structure; the Ready chip
  while a structure is ready (until P130).
- **Drawer (P120, P120b):** side panel width 312-420 dp and full safe height; bottom sheet half the
  height; at least two card columns everywhere (upright three on the 6-inch phone and at 360 dp,
  four from about 450 dp wide); on `web_phone_small` the grid scrolls vertically (84 dp left for
  it); the economy in the head and the pill hidden; card states from crafted snapshots, each with
  its shape; a tap queues one `QUEUE`; a long-press sends as many `QUEUE`s as room and credits
  allow, at most `queue_hold_count` (ADR 0025 question 2 A); a tap on a queue item sends `CANCEL
  [tab, slot]` for that slot, and a second tap before the next snapshot sends nothing; "Needs
  <name>" on a locked card.
- **Pill (P119, P119b):** low power look and the word; a credits fall shows "-N" for
  `economy_flash_ms` and counts down, a rise "+N"; the income per minute from crafted
  `stat_harvested` samples; no text set per frame at rest (a counter on the label's setter).
- **Radar (P121):** the scope's crop centre follows the camera's ground centre; the crop is in the
  iso projection (a cell north-east of the centre draws up and right); the frame rect matches
  `visible_world_rect` scaled; the scope redraws only when the camera or the picture moved; the
  full map's jump and P88b's long-press `MOVE` unchanged.
- **Cues (P123):** a crafted `SHOT` on an own row outside the view raises one toast, one rim wedge
  in the right screen direction and one ping; a second inside `attack_cue_area_tiles` within
  `attack_cue_ms` raises none; with the full map open only the ping; a tap on the toast jumps the
  camera.
- **Command bar and groups (P122b, P122):** the bar shows Deselect at its start, the chip, and
  Stop for units and no order for a building; there is no Attack button; Stop = one `STOP`; a
  ground tap is a `MOVE` and an enemy tap an `ATTACK`, as today; Deselect empties the selection
  for units, an own building and an enemy building, sends no Command and hides the bar, while
  during placement and the strike aim it empties the selection and the bar keeps its slot (rect
  equality for BUILD, the strike button and its check mark and X); "+" saves into the lowest free
  group; a hold of `group_assign_ms` overwrites; empty groups hidden.
- **Pause (P124):** `paused` set and cleared; Restart's, Surrender's and Exit's confirms; the
  Vibration switch writes the test's own `user://` file and every `vibrate` call obeys it; no node
  of an ad class in `scenes/main.tscn` (the admob-policy guard).
- **View only (P125):** a scripted main-scene run with every HUD action (unfolds, a pause, a
  Deselect) ends with the same state hash as the same Commands
  without them (P62's method).
- **ADR 0026 (P128-P131):** `PLACE [type]` drops for each failed rule (tab, a build time under 1,
  producer complete and online, faction, requirement complete, credits, the site limit, the
  ground) and charges the whole price when it applies; a site's `build_left` falls by
  `Power.scaled` per tick, pauses while it is offline or its owner defeated, and goes on after its
  Headquarters dies (ADR 0026 question 4 A); its hp steps sum to `type_max_hp` for a site never
  hit; completion starts the charge, spawns a refinery's tanker and counts `stat_built`; an
  unfinished row makes and uses no power, is no producer, meets no requirement, does not fire or
  strike, takes no tanker and keeps nobody alive; `CANCEL_BUILD` refunds the share, frees the
  tiles in REMOVE, counts no loss and marks its DIED record; the data rule rejects a Structures
  type with `build_ticks` 0; GDScript and native impls give equal hashes over a scripted match with
  sites (the existing both-impl check); the dummy places first and its smoke matches end within
  bounds; after P131 `QUEUE` of a structure and an empty `PLACE` are dropped.

## Risks
- **The rewrite of the layout suites** is the bulk of the work (ADR 0025's Consequences); each row
  rewrites the suites of the parts it replaces, never two rows the same suite.
- **`scenes/main.tscn` contention** across G, G2, G3 and I: serialised merges, one combined-tree
  check before merging rows that wait for the same merge.
- **The fonts:** glyph-cache hitches on first use, the web build's size, `tnum` missing (P118
  checks; fallback named), the base-px tracking's spread across phones (P118 checks the band).
- **The orange accent near danger red** for Derrick League players: the hue rule and never colour
  alone.
- **Short landscape screens** (`web_phone_small`, 280 dp safe height): the group column takes two
  columns of two with the bar; the full map goes left of the right column when the strike button
  is shown (ADR 0025 §2's table); the drawer's grid scrolls.
- **ADR 0026's balance:** sites can be killed while they rise; the dummy loses some; the smoke shows
  whether match lengths move; Phase 4's balance runs take it from there.
- **The Deselect button next to the screen edge** in landscape: a miss lands on the edge or the
  read-only chip, never on an order; the phone check of P122 confirms the thumb reach.
