# Plan: HUD v2 (the corner HUD) and place-first construction

Date: 2026-11-01. Base: `main` at 5203ce0 (ADR 0024 accepted; P85g #256, P86c #257 and P85f #258
merged). Decisions: `docs/adr/0025-hud-v2-corner-hud-build-drawer-radar-scope.md` and
`docs/adr/0026-place-first-construction.md`, both **proposed**, with eleven and five questions for
Michael. Phase: 3 (the battle-screen HUD of ROADMAP Phase 3, boxes 2 and 3), with the start page
pointed to Phase 5 and its banner to Phase 6. Rows in the style of
`docs/plans/2026-10-22-phase-3.md`, which lists them as pointers to this plan.

**Status.** Proposed. Nothing here starts before the lead's docs row P26ap records Michael's
answers. The rows are written to the recommended answers; where an answer would change a row, the
row says so.

## Goal
Michael's HUD design of 2026-11-01: in battle only four corner anchors, everything else unfolds on
demand and folds away on a map touch, the same parts in landscape and portrait (only the drawer's
side changes), our names, our rules (ADR 0025 §15); and, after the HUD, structures placed first and
built on the spot (ADR 0026).

## Out of scope
- The start page (ADR 0025 §12, question 7 A): Phase 5's main menu row (pointer P126 below). Its
  banner: Phase 6 (pointer P127).
- Left-handed switch (question 8 A): Phase 5's settings; the layout is mirror-ready from P119.
- Settings, Sound and Music in the pause menu (no settings screen, no audio yet).
- Guard, Patrol, Scatter, Repair, Sell, Primary, repeat production, Campaign, Continue,
  Multiplayer, map region names, a radar that goes offline, pay-later queues.
- Any sim change outside ADR 0026's rows (P128, P128b, P131).

## Test plan (what is proven headless)
gdUnit4 at the six sizes of `tests/support/screen_sizes.gd`, the portrait profiles, the short
landscape profiles (`web_phone_small` 800 x 304 dp, `web_phone` 914 x 355 dp) and a new profile
with the design's insets (`ui_profiles.notch_design`: landscape 59 sides / 21 bottom, portrait 59
top / 34 bottom). No timing run; the perf suites only inside `verify.sh`.
- **Layout (P119 on):** `HudCorners.place` as a pure function on crafted inputs: every rect inside
  the safe rect, no two overlapping, every tappable rect at least 48 dp a side (BUILD 64, groups 56,
  orders 64 high), mirrored and not, with and without the command bar, the strike button, the full
  map and the toast; the group column's second column on `web_phone_small`; the portrait BUILD
  lift (8 dp above the bar). In the scene: the nodes take those rects, at each size and after a turn.
- **Unfold rules (P119, P120, P121, P119b, P124):** opening one unfold closes the other; a tap, a
  double-tap, a long-press, a pan and a pinch on the map close the drawer, the unpinned full map
  and the economy card and still do their work (a `MOVE` queued, a selection made); a touch on a
  HUD control closes nothing but the economy card; the pinned full map stays; `radar_close_ms`
  with stepped frame time closes it, a touch on it restarts the count; pause takes every touch;
  a turn keeps the state; a new match starts with nothing unfolded.
- **BUILD (P120):** from crafted snapshots: the ring's progress is the first-finishing head's, the
  badge the item count, "stalled" (grey, "!") when no head advanced between two snapshots with a
  queue not empty, not on low power; the Ready chip while a structure is ready (until P130).
- **Drawer (P120, P120b):** side panel width 312-420 dp and full safe height; bottom sheet half the
  height; at least two card columns everywhere (upright three on the 6-inch phone and at 360 dp, four from
  about 450 dp wide);
  the economy in the head and the pill hidden; card states from crafted snapshots, each with its
  shape; a tap queues one `QUEUE`; a long-press sends up to five `QUEUE`s, never more than room or
  credits allow (question 2 A); a tap on a queue item sends `CANCEL [tab, slot]` for that slot;
  "Needs <name>" on a locked card.
- **Pill (P119, P119b):** low power look and the word; a credits fall shows "-N" for
  `economy_flash_ms` and counts down, a rise "+N"; the income per minute from crafted
  `stat_harvested` samples; no text set per frame at rest (a counter on the label's setter).
- **Radar (P121):** the scope's crop centre follows the camera's ground centre; the frame rect
  matches `visible_world_rect` scaled; the scope redraws only when the camera or the picture moved;
  the full map's jump and P88b's long-press `MOVE` unchanged.
- **Cues (P123):** a crafted `SHOT` on an own row outside the view raises one toast, one rim wedge in
  the right direction and one ping; a second inside `attack_cue_area_tiles` within `attack_cue_ms`
  raises none; a tap on the toast jumps the camera.
- **Command bar and groups (P122, P122b):** the bar shows only Attack and Stop for units and no
  order for a building; Attack armed + ground tap = one `ATTACK_MOVE` and disarms; an enemy tap is
  an `ATTACK`; Stop = one `STOP`; a ground tap unarmed is a `MOVE`; "+" saves into the lowest free
  group; a hold of `group_assign_ms` overwrites; empty groups hidden.
- **Pause (P124):** `paused` set and cleared; Surrender's and Exit's confirms; the Vibration switch
  writes the test's own `user://` file and every `vibrate` call obeys it; no node of an ad class in
  `scenes/main.tscn` (the admob-policy guard).
- **View only (P125):** a scripted main-scene run with every HUD action (unfolds, a pause, Attack
  armed and disarmed without a ground tap) ends with the same state hash as the same Commands
  without them (P62's method).
- **ADR 0026 (P128-P131):** `PLACE [type]` drops for each failed rule (tab, producer complete and
  online, faction, requirement complete, credits, a site under way, the ground) and charges the
  whole price when it applies; a site's `build_left` falls by `Power.scaled` per tick and pauses
  without a complete online Headquarters, while defeated or offline; its hp steps sum to
  `type_max_hp` for a site never hit; completion starts the charge, spawns a refinery's tanker and
  counts `stat_built`; an unfinished row makes and uses no power, is no producer, meets no
  requirement, does not fire, takes no tanker and keeps nobody alive; `CANCEL_BUILD` refunds the
  share, frees the tiles in REMOVE, counts no loss and marks its DIED record; GDScript and native
  impls give equal hashes over a scripted match with sites (the existing both-impl check); the
  dummy places first and wins the base race it won before within the smoke's bounds; after P131
  `QUEUE` of a structure and an empty `PLACE` are dropped.

## Risks
- **The rewrite of the layout suites** is the bulk of the work (ADR 0025's Consequences); each row
  rewrites the suites of the parts it replaces, never two rows the same suite.
- **`scenes/main.tscn` contention** across G, G2, G3 and I: serialised merges, one combined-tree
  check before merging rows that wait for the same merge.
- **The fonts:** glyph-cache hitches on first use, the web build's size, `tnum` missing (P118
  checks; fallback named).
- **The orange accent near danger red** for Derrick League players: the hue rule and never colour
  alone.
- **Short landscape screens** (304 dp high): the group column's second column; the full map's 162 dp
  plus its head under the scope fits (274 dp of 288).
- **ADR 0026's balance:** sites can be killed while they rise; the dummy loses some; the smoke shows
  whether match lengths move; Phase 4's balance runs take it from there.
- **Michael's answers** can change P120b (question 2), P122b (3), P89/P123 (9), P85f2 (1) and
  P128-P131 (ADR 0026's 1-5); each row names its branch.
