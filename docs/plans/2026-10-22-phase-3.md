## Goal
Every Phase 3 box in `docs/ROADMAP.md` is met on `main`:
- The six GDD buildings exist, are placed with a ghost (valid and invalid tint, confirm and
  cancel), block their tiles, have power, and can be destroyed.
- A build sidebar with three tabs, queues, cost and progress, a power bar and low-power effects
  works in landscape and portrait (ADR 0015 §5), with the minimap in it.
- Ore fields regrow; harvesters run the loop to the refinery; credits rise.
- All six MVP units can be produced, and rally points send them on.
- A match ends in victory, defeat or a draw, with a result screen.
- A dummy opponent builds a base and defends it, through Commands like a player.
- The portrait box: the battle screen follows ADR 0015 §5 with the real sidebar band; the Android
  build follows the sensor through `docs/plans/2026-10-19-portrait.md`'s last row, P64.

Then Michael runs the gate ("Gate procedure"): a full match against the dummy on his phone, and the
perf budget still met on two cards, the Phase 2 perf arena and the new base arena (ADR 0021).

## Sim data model and Command changes
ADR 0017 fixes the layout and ADR 0018 the rules. The hard rules hold for every row of this table:
integers only (credits, ore, progress in hundredths of a tick, milli-tile positions); 15 Hz; no
random draw anywhere in Phase 3's sim rules; every change through a Command or a sim rule on hashed
state; every number in `data/*.tres`; the view reads only the snapshot. Loops visit players, tabs,
rows (ascending id) and ore cells (ascending) in a fixed order.

| Topic | State | Commands | Phase | Hash (ADR 0016) | Kernels (ADR 0013) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buildings as rows | rows with a footprint type (speed 0, radius 0, armour `BUILDING`); `World.add_building` blocks `move_cost`, bumps `map_version`, invalidates the flow cache; `remove_dead` unblocks | `PLACE` (10) | COMMANDS, REMOVE | rows as today; `move_cost` through the map digest | TARGETING (both twins, P71): an `ATTACK` lock on a building keeps the approach point that `Sim._apply_attack` writes (ADR 0017 §1); the others unchanged; a building-world case in every kernel lockstep (P71) |
| Placement validity | `Placement.reason` over `PlacementInput` (map, terrain, `terrain_buildable`, `move_cost`, `explored`, rows) | `PLACE` | COMMANDS | reads hashed state only | none |
| Power | `power_made`, `power_used` per player | - | ECONOMY | derived, out, rebuild test | `Combat.fire` (GDScript) skips offline defences; TARGETING unchanged |
| Ore fields | `ore_cell` (map), `ore_amount` | - | ECONOMY (regrowth) | `ore_cell` in the digest, `ore_amount` hashed | none |
| Harvesters | `harvest_mode`, `cargo`, `harvest_slot`, `home` (row columns) | `HARVEST` (14); unit-group orders set IDLE | ECONOMY | hashed | none (orders as `Sim._apply_point` gives them, through `Movement.reset_nav`) |
| Refinery | building with a dock and a free harvester | `PLACE` | COMMANDS | as rows | none |
| Credits | `credits` per player | `QUEUE` pays, `CANCEL` refunds | COMMANDS, ECONOMY (unload) | hashed | none |
| Production queues | `queue_type`, `queue_count`, `queue_progress` per player and tab | `QUEUE` (11), `CANCEL` (12) | PRODUCTION | hashed | none |
| Rally points | `rally_x`, `rally_y` per player and tab | `RALLY` (13) | COMMANDS, PRODUCTION | hashed | none |
| Victory and defeat | `defeated_tick`, `had_producer`, `stat_*` per player; `match_end_tick`, `winner` (ticks stored as `tick + 1`, 0 = playing or running) | `SURRENDER` (20) | PRODUCTION (`Outcome.run`) | hashed | none |
| Last seen buildings | `ghost_*` records per player (World, not `FogGrid`) | - | MEMORY | hashed, live entries | none |
| Derived lists | `building_rows`, `harvester_rows` | - | ECONOMY | derived, out, rebuild test | none |

- **Kernels:** the new phases start as GDScript; the lead applies ADR 0013 §1's clauses at P93's

## View and touch UX
ADR 0019 decides the UI; ADR 0015 §5 the layout in both orientations. In short:
- **Sidebar** (P85, P85b): right zone in landscape, bottom band in portrait (20 %, at least 200
  dp); credits and power bar, three tabs (icon-only in the band), item tiles of at least 64 dp with
  cost, progress, count and a state shape (available, locked, unaffordable, ready). Tap queues, a
  long-press cancels one (refund), a ready structure opens placement. P85 sets the portrait zone's
  height through P61's one place, so the ArmyBar moves above the band; the bottom stack stays at
  most 40 % of the safe height, the band giving way first.
- **Placement** (P86): ghost at the view centre's tile; green with a solid outline or red with a
  hatch and a reason glyph, from `Placement.reason` on the snapshot; a drag that starts on the ghost
  moves it 64 dp above the finger (ADR 0019 question 1: B), other drags pan; check mark and
  X of 64 dp in the sidebar zone; pending and "Blocked" states; a turn keeps the ghost's tile and
  sends nothing.
- **Map** (P84, P84b, P87): buildings with team layers and health bars; ore cells by amount;
  harvesters; selecting an own building opens its tab; a ground tap with a barracks or factory
  selected sets the rally flag; harvesters plus a tap on ore harvest; "All army" and mixed boxes
  leave harvesters out.
- **Minimap** (P88): top of the sidebar in landscape, right end of the band in portrait; one
  texture at fog cadence; tap or drag jumps the camera, a long-press with units selected moves them.
- **Low power and under attack** (P89): dimmed consumers, offline glyph on defences, a pulsing
  power bar; edge arrows and minimap pings for own rows hit off screen.
- **Result screen and surrender** (P90): Victory, Defeat or Draw with the per-player stats;
  "Play again" and "Back"; a "Surrender" button with a confirm dialog at the top left.
- **Where a match starts** (P90): Dev → Skirmish (ADR 0019 question 4). The preview's main scene
  keeps `data/scenarios/controls_test.tres`, so the Phase 1 and 2 scene suites keep theirs.
- Every UI row tests at the six sizes of ADR 0015 §6 and the portrait profiles, with screenshots
  in the PR.

## Dummy opponent
ADR 0020: `DummyAi` (ai-dev) reads only a `PlayerView`, thinks every 15 ticks on a tick whose step
does not hash, inside `TickJob.run` before `loop.step` (on the worker in `WORKER`), and appends
Commands that `MatchLoop` stamps and records. It follows `data/ai/dummy.tres`: a build order,
a fixed placement spiral around its Headquarters, a harvester target, and a defence radius it never
leaves. It never attacks. No random number, no bonus, no hidden information.

  units never enter a footprint and a building's position never changes over 900 ticks with units

- The artillery check (P68c, from P69's review). Artillery's weapon has `min_range` 3 000 (ADR 0018
  §8), and the approach point of an `ATTACK` on a building is the centre of a ring cell, 2 000 to
  2 829 from a 3x3 centre and closer for the smaller footprints (ADR 0017 §1), so it lies inside
  3 000 for every building: it is never a spot artillery can fire from. What happens instead
  follows from `main` and ADR 0017 §1 ("Range and the HOLD at range still measure to the target's
  position (the footprint centre)"):
  - `Targeting._engage` puts a row in `HOLD` when its target stands at
    `min_range^2 <= d2 <= range^2`, measured to the target's position and whatever the goal, and
    `Combat.fire` fires in the same band without needing `HOLD` or a clear line. An artillery unit
    ordered to `ATTACK` a building from beyond 9 000 walks towards its approach point; its distance
    to the centre changes by at most one step and one separation push per tick, far less than the
    6 000 of the band, so it is inside 3 000..9 000 on some tick before it can be inside 3 000. It
    fires by the tick after its distance first falls to 9 000 or less (`Combat.fire` reads the
    positions after the move, `_engage` those at the start of the next tick), holds from then on,
    and never reaches its approach point. The `ATTACK` lock needs the player to see the building's
    cell (P46b); `_unit_problems` keeps weapon `range` <= `acquire_range` <= `sight_range`, so the
    artillery's own sight reaches about that far (the sight masks round to cells), and an `ATTACK`
    that loses sight becomes `ATTACK_MOVE` to the goal it left (the approach point), whose
    acquisition takes the building again in the same band.
  - An artillery unit already inside 3 000 of the centre does not fire and does not back away (the
    Phase 2 rule: "A target inside `min_range` is not in range, and no unit backs away from it");
    it walks to its approach point, also inside 3 000, and waits there. Known limit, "Risks".
  - So the HOLD-at-range rule holds as ADR 0017 §1 states it. Its consequence "the approach point
    is always within range" holds for the maximum range only: for artillery the approach point is
    inside `min_range` ("Risks"). No ADR changes. P71 pins the first behaviour
    because it is the first row with an artillery and a building in one World: `add_building`
    lands here, and its suites build types by hand as the Phase 2 suites do; P72 (lane B, after
    P69 only) adds `artillery.tres` but changes no `game/sim` code and may merge before P71, so no
    World of P72 is sure to hold a building row.
- Acceptance, artillery (P68c): a hand-built type with ADR 0018 §8's numbers (speed 67, a shell

## Risks
- **Flow-field churn:** every placement and building death invalidates all cached fields; with
  frequent building the FLOW slice may lag and units start late. The base arena measures it; a
  local invalidation would need a new ADR.
- **Map digest after a placement:** the next checkpoint re-hashes the map (about 16 KB); rare, so
  outside the checkpoint p95, but visible on a card as a spike; ADR 0016's line is read on both
  cards.
- **Harvester traffic:** jams at a dock or on a small field can stall harvesters (Movement's stall
  rule ends an order short of the goal); the retry and resume rules bound it, and P75 tests four
  harvesters per refinery.
- **Rows and caps:** 2 x (75 + 40) = 230 of `max_rows` 256; the refinery's free harvester counts
  against the 75 (ADR 0018 §3), so the bound holds; a row-full tick holds production rather than
  refusing a spawn, and a data test keeps the sum in range.
- **Draw calls:** buildings, ore, flags and the minimap add layers against `draw_calls_max` 60;
  P84 and P88 keep ADR 0011 §3's draw rules (atlases, one minimap texture), and only the base-arena
  card can measure the count (P93c).
- **Main thread:** the sidebar's tiles and the minimap update from the snapshot; a per-frame
  rebuild would show in `work_p95_ms`; both redraw only on change.
- **The AI on the worker:** a slow think lands in the job p95 on 1 tick in 15; the dummy's work is
  bounded and counted, and Phase 4 keeps the ai-dev 1 ms rule.
- **Portrait band:** on short screens the band gives way first (ADR 0015 §5); the placement check
  mark and X live in the band, so P86 tests them at the smallest portrait profile.
- **Sidebar gestures:** a long-press to cancel and a sideways scroll share the icon row; the scroll
  wins once the finger moves beyond `tap_slop_dp`.
- **Dependencies outside this plan:** P85 waits for P61, P86 for P62, box 3 and the gate for P64.
  On 757b5f9 the portrait rows P59 (#164) and P60 (#165) are open PRs and P61-P63 have started,
  stacked on P60. The Phase 2 gate passed (issue #157, ROADMAP Phase 2 box 7 ticked by P26ag,
  #162) and P65 merged (#163), so nothing in Phase 2 holds a Phase 3 row any more. On aafbde2
  (P68c) P59-P63 have merged (#164, #165, #167-#169); P64 also waits for the portrait plan's
  amendment row P63b (#170).
- **Artillery inside its minimum range** (P68c): an artillery unit ordered to `ATTACK` a building
  from closer than 3 000 to its centre does not fire and does not back away (the Phase 2 rule of
  `Targeting._engage`); it walks to its approach point, which lies within 2 829 of the centre, and
  waits there. Ordered from farther out it holds and fires at range first (P71). Accepted for
  Phase 3: the player moves it out by hand. Phase 4's balance runs may revisit it (a back-away
  rule, or an approach point on the `min_range` ring for a weapon that has one), through a new
  ADR, because ADR 0017 §1 fixes the approach point.
- **Scope:** anything beyond "builds and defends" for the dummy is Phase 4; the out-of-scope list
  holds.
- **Legal:** buildings, units, the map and the art are original; the art rows describe each
  silhouette before drawing it; no name or look from an existing game.
