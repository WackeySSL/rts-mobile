## Goal
- The game opens on its own splash (the app icon), the icon's tank drives while the game loads,
  and the main menu opens out of the icon. Back on Android pops a screen; at the root it asks to
  quit.
- Main menu, map select, settings (sound, music, vibration, reduced effects, privacy options),
  How to play, Licences, all in portrait and landscape at ADR 0015 §6's six sizes.
- Sound effects for combat, units, buildings and the UI, and three music pieces, all licensed CC0,
  CC-BY or made in this repo, with credits; volumes saved.
- Commander rank from XP, unlocks, one local save file; a running match survives the app being
  killed in the background (restored by replaying its log).
- An interactive tutorial (about 3 minutes on first launch, replayable) and a How to play screen
  that together cover every entry of `docs/HOW_TO_PLAY.md`, proven by a test.
- Maps 2 and 3; the balance run's median match 8-10 minutes.
- The 150-unit budget holds with audio on.

## Out of scope (each needs a plan change and Michael's yes)
Voice acting and a narrator (GDD: radio chirps); a composed theme (later); a campaign; multiplayer;
Headquarters skins (ADR 0045 §4, answer 4); left-handed mode (ADR 0025 §14, answer 11);
translations beyond English (answer 12: the keys only); stored replays in `user://replays/`;
an animated match-start loader (answer 10: P536's still card instead); cloud save.

## Command, state and data changes
- **Commands, events, layout:** none. `STATE_FORMAT` stays 7.
- **Setup:** `MatchSetup.bonus_starting_credits` (Phase 6's starting-credits grant) becomes a
  per-player setup value like `income_percent`: `WorldLoad.set_bonus_credits` adds it to the
  player's starting credits before `MatchLoop.create`, clamped to `RulesDef.bonus_credits_max`;
  `Replay` `FORMAT` 3 records it and `apply_setup` puts it back. At 0 the state hash and every
  golden stay. The new rule int follows the `RulesDef` pattern (a `World` member,
  `WorldShape.RULE_SCALARS` and `UNHASHED_SCALARS`, the pinned rule count 48 -> 49) and changes
  `defs_hash`, which no literal pins; old resume files are then refused, as any data change does.
- **Data:** every number of the new systems in `data/*.tres` (volumes, voice caps, cooldowns,
  crossfade and duck, intro min and max, XP and rank tables, tutorial timings). View and meta data
  are not in `defs_hash`. P509 keeps them from starting `balance.yml`.

## Test plan (what is proven headless)
- **Determinism and resume (ADR 0042 §6):** a match played to tick N, written by `ResumeStore`,
  restored by `Restorer` and played on to M has the uninterrupted run's checkpoint hashes, under
  both implementations and both tick modes, with the AI on its thread; a tampered log, another
  `defs_hash` or another app version is refused and deleted. P512's state-hash-neutral test at 0.
- **Save (ADR 0042 §1-§4):** atomic write, a hash-valid temp loads first, a corrupt main file
  falls back to `.bak`, a newer version is never overwritten, v0 (`settings.cfg`) migrates,
  unknown sections survive.
- **Audio (ADR 0041):** the dummy audio driver headless; cue choice, voice caps, coalescing and the
  per-tick budget on scripted snapshots; **no cue for an event the local player cannot see** (the
  fog leak test); no allocation per tick in `BattleSounds` (the existing guard pattern); mute
  reasons stack (background, ad).
- **Intro (ADR 0043 §2):** a fake clock and fake loads: never ends before the loads and consent,
  never before `min_ms`, holds after `max_ms`, a tap skips the motion, reduced gives the fade.
- **Menus:** stack push, pop and back; every screen at the six sizes (inside the safe rect, 48 dp,
  no overlap, the banner band reserved: 16 dp floor, 48 dp on the start page); a turn keeps the
  stack. `AdService.fake_auto_close` keeps every awaited Phase 6 flow from blocking.
- **Coverage (ADR 0044 §5):** every `HOW_TO_PLAY.md` row has a help entry; every Teach row is in a
  tutorial beat; no stale ids; no developer references (backticks, `data/`, `.tres`) in player
  text.
- **Tutorial:** `TutorialBot` plays every lesson to its end headless through the player's own entry
  points; each condition has a unit test; the lesson's beat timings sum within its budget.
- **Maps:** reachability per move class, two safe and two contested fields per base (GDD), seat
  symmetry (ADR 0033); smoke matches on each map.
- **Mutation testing:** P512 and P515 only (about 10 chosen mutants each).
- **Not in a session:** timings (the restore's ticks per second is a CI `PERF` line, P516b) and
  balance runs (GitHub Actions).
