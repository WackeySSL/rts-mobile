# Plan: Portrait and landscape (ADR 0015)

Date: 2026-10-19. Decision: `docs/adr/0015-portrait-and-landscape.md` (Michael's decision of
2026-10-19 and the lead's frame decisions of the same day). Docs PR: P26v. Base: `main` at
`32bfb89`. Each PR branches from `main` after its dependencies have merged.

## Goal
The whole game is playable in portrait as well as landscape, with the same dp sizes on the same
phone, and the player may rotate during a match (ADR 0015 §1). This plan covers what exists now:
the base size, the safe area, the test sizes, the battle-screen HUD, the camera and gestures, the
dev scenes, and the orientation flip. The build sidebar (Phase 3), the menus (Phase 5) and the ad
and consent screens (Phase 6) are built for both orientations in their own phases, by the rule of
ADR 0015 §5; their criteria are in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

## Affected modules
- `game/ui/` (new autoload script, `UiScale`'s orientation function, `SafeAreaMargin`'s comment,
  `ArmyBar`, `SelectionPanel`, `touch_probe.gd`, `dev_menu.gd`), `scenes/main.tscn`,
  `scenes/dev/dev_menu.tscn`, `scenes/dev/touch_probe.tscn` (touch-ux-dev).
- `game/view/RtsCamera.gd`, `game/input/TouchInput.gd` (touch-ux-dev).
- `project.godot` (the autoload entry in P59; the orientation in P64), `scripts/export-android.sh`,
  `scripts/ci/test-export-notices.sh` (release-engineer, P64).
- `tests/support/`, `tests/ui/`, `tests/scenes/`, `tests/view/`, `tests/input/`, `tests/project/`.
- `docs/HOW_TO_PLAY.md` (P61, P64), `docs/ROADMAP.md` (ticks), `CLAUDE.md`, the `touch-ui-rules`
  skill and the `touch-ux-dev` agent (P64 removes the "landscape-locked until P64" clause).
- Unchanged: `game/sim/`, `native/`, `game/core/`, `game/ai/`, `data/` (no setting gets a portrait
  value, ADR 0015 §3), Commands, replays, the state hash.

## Command and data changes
None. Orientation is view-only (ADR 0015 §1). No Command type, no field, no `.tres` value changes.
A rotation queues no Command (proven in P62).

## Test plan (what is proven headless)
- The stretch at the six sizes of ADR 0015 §6 (landscape 1280x720, 2400x1080, 2048x1536;
  portrait 720x1280, 1080x2400, 1536x2048) and at the portrait profiles matches the engine with
  the base-size swap, and a portrait profile gives the same `dp(48)` as its landscape twin (P59).
- Every `size_changed` handler ends in the same state after the double emission of a flip as after
  one call (P59).
- Every UI and layout suite reads the shared size list, and none is landscape-only once P64 merges
  (P60's scan).
- At every size, every HUD control is inside the safe rect, at least 48 dp on each axis, overlaps
  no other control and stays out of the reserved sidebar zone (P61, P63).
- The camera keeps its centre and world px per dp across a turn, within the map clamp (P62).
- A rotation in the middle of a pan, a box or a pinch queues no Command and leaves the state hash
  at a fixed tick unchanged (P62).
- The orientation setting, the manifest check and the web preset are pinned (P64).
- Not provable headless: the real Android rotation (no activity restart, the cutout side, the
  `fullUser` behaviour with auto-rotate off), the SDK screens of Phase 6. P64's phone check covers
  the first three on Michael's phone.

### P59 ui: base size per orientation and the safe area in portrait (touch-ux-dev)
- Why: ADR 0015 §2 and §3. Without it a 1080x2400 window runs at stretch 0.84375 instead of 1.5.
- Files:
  - `game/ui/ui_orientation_service.gd` (new; autoload `UiOrientation`, no `class_name`): sets
    `get_tree().root.content_scale_size` in `_ready()` and on the root viewport's `size_changed`
    to the base size of the window's orientation, only when it differs. The landscape base is read
    from `display/window/size/viewport_width` and `viewport_height`; the portrait base is its
    transpose.
  - `game/ui/UiScale.gd`: one pure static function for the orientation rule (height > width is
    portrait; the name is the author's, shown in the PR) and one for the base size of a window
    size, both used by the autoload; no other change.
  - `game/ui/SafeAreaMargin.gd`: the doc comment says "a 180 degree turn" without
    "sensor-landscape"; the rule stays.
  - `project.godot`: the `[autoload]` entry `UiOrientation="*res://game/ui/ui_orientation_service.gd"`
    after `CommandQueue`.
  - Tests: `tests/project/test_autoloads.gd` (the entry), `tests/support/ui_profiles.gd` (the four
    portrait profiles of ADR 0015 §6 behind a new `portrait()`, so `all()` and every suite that
    loops over it stay landscape until their row; its header formula per orientation),
    `tests/ui/test_ui_scale.gd` (its engine case covers `all()` and `portrait()`; `EXPECTED_DP48`
    gains `android_6in_portrait` 84, `web_tablet_portrait` 60, `desktop_portrait` 48,
    `web_phone_portrait` 96), and `tests/ui/test_ui_orientation.gd` (new).
- Acceptance:
  - At 1080x2400 the engine's stretch is 1.5 and the visible rect 720x1600 base px; at 1536x2048
    1.6 and 960x1280; at 720x1280 1.0; every landscape profile and size gives today's value.
  - Each portrait profile's `dp(48)` equals its landscape twin's where one exists
    (`android_6in`, `web_tablet`, `desktop`).
  - The orientation function: 1080x2400 portrait, 2400x1080 landscape, 1000x1000 landscape.
  - Flipping 2400x1080 → 1080x2400 → 2400x1080 leaves a `DpMinSize`, a `SafeAreaMargin`, a
    `TouchInput` (its recognizer's slop in px) and an `ArmyBar` in the same state as a fresh
    scene at the end size (the double emission is harmless).
  - `SafeAreaMargin` in portrait with a top cutout (`android_6in_portrait` with a safe area of
    `Rect2i(0, 100, 1080, 2237)`): top and bottom get the larger inset plus `margin_dp`.
- Phone check (web preview, which already turns): turn the phone in the dev menu; the buttons keep
  their size instead of shrinking.

- Files: `tests/support/screen_sizes.gd` (new: `LANDSCAPE` = 1280x720, 2400x1080, 2048x1536;

  names the row that removes it (P61, P62 or P63). Every rule has a fixture line it must flag.
- Acceptance: no case changes what it runs (the same sizes as before, landscape only); the scan
  is green with the pending list and red on each fixture.
- Phone check: none (tests only).

### P61 ui: the battle-screen HUD in portrait (touch-ux-dev)
- Why: ADR 0015 §5 (layout table, risks, limits).
- Files: `game/ui/SelectionPanel.gd` (`_fit_band` places the panel by orientation: today's band in
  landscape; in portrait the top line right of the top-left controls, one line with the type rows
  side by side, names only when the whole line fits), `game/ui/ArmyBar.gd` or `scenes/main.tscn`
  (in portrait the ArmyBar sits bottom-left above the reserved bottom zone's top edge, which is the
  safe bottom edge while the zone is empty), the reserved zone by orientation (right in
  landscape, bottom in portrait: 20 % of the long axis and at least 200 dp), `scenes/main.tscn`,
  `docs/HOW_TO_PLAY.md` (the selection panel row: "at the bottom centre; at the top in portrait"),
  and the suites `tests/ui/test_selection_panel.gd`, `tests/ui/test_army_bar.gd`,
  `tests/ui/test_touch_target_sizes.gd`, `tests/scenes/test_main_scene.gd` (to `ScreenSizes.ALL`;
  their `PORTRAIT_PENDING` entries go).
- Size: about 250 production lines. Split trigger: if the first commit passes 400, P61b takes the
  SelectionPanel's one-line portrait layout and P61 keeps the placement and the zone.
- Acceptance, at all six sizes and the portrait profiles:
  - every HUD control is inside the safe rect, at least 48 dp on each axis, and overlaps no other;
  - nothing but the ArmyBar sits in the bottom part in portrait, and no HUD control enters the
    reserved zone (right in landscape, bottom in portrait);
  - the bottom stack in portrait (zone plus the ArmyBar line) is at most 40 % of the safe height;
  - the SelectionPanel is one row high in portrait with one, two and three types selected, and
    its names hide all together when the line is too narrow;
  - the landscape layout is unchanged (today's cases pass as they are).
- Phone check (web preview): select tanks and a mixed group, turn the phone: the panel moves to
  the top in one line, the army bar stays bottom-left, and every button keeps its size.

### P62 view: the camera and gestures on rotation (touch-ux-dev)
- Why: ADR 0015 §3 (gestures) and §4 (camera).
- Files: `game/view/RtsCamera.gd` (the last-seen orientation and base px per dp; on a change:
  stop the snap, keep `position`, rescale the zoom by the `bppd` ratio within the pinch bounds,
  clamp), `game/input/TouchInput.gd` (`_recognizer.cancel_all()` on an orientation change),
  `tests/view/test_rts_camera.gd`, `tests/input/test_touch_input.gd`, and a scene case for the
  view-only proof (`tests/scenes/test_controls_flow.gd` or a new suite).
- Acceptance:
  - A turn 2400x1080 → 1080x2400 keeps `zoom` exactly and `position` where the clamp allows; a
    centre near a map edge moves only as far as the new clamp needs; `limit_*` is unchanged.
  - With a profile pair whose stretch differs across the turn (`web_phone` 2400x932 →
    `web_phone_portrait`-like 1080x2100 window), world px per dp before and after are equal
    within 0.1 %, and the zoom stays within the pinch bounds.
  - A snap in flight at the turn is stopped and not resumed.
  - A turn during a pan, a box and a pinch emits `CAMERA_END` or `BOX_CANCEL` and no `TAP`.
  - The main scene run 60 ticks with a turn at tick 30 during each gesture queues no Command and
    has the same `last_hash` at tick 60 as without the turn.
- Phone check (web preview): pan to a corner, turn the phone: the same point stays in the middle
  unless the map edge stops it, and the tanks keep their size.

## Risks
- **The double `size_changed` on a flip** (ADR 0015 Context): a handler that is not idempotent
  breaks only on rotation. P59's case covers today's handlers; every later handler joins it.
- **Narrow map view in portrait** (ADR 0015 §5): the same zoom levels show 11.25 tiles across at
  zoom 1.0 on the 6-inch phone. If P64's phone check finds it too narrow, a wider level is a data
  change to `zoom_levels` for both orientations; a portrait-only level would need a new ADR.
- **The web preview turns before the APK does**: between P59 and P61 the portrait battle screen
  on the preview has the landscape HUD placement at the right size. Expected; not a bug.
- **Android system bars**: if the window is not the exact transpose after a turn, the stretch
  differs slightly; §4's zoom rescale and `UiScale` absorb it, and P64's phone check looks at it.
- **Scope**: the build sidebar, menus and ad screens are not in these rows; their phases build
  them for both orientations (ROADMAP Phase 3, 5 and 6 criteria).
