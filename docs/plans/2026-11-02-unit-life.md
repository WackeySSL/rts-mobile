# Plan: unit life and effects

Date: 2026-11-02. Base: `main` at 3c29b16 (P128b merged; HUD v2 lanes running). Decisions:
`docs/adr/0027-unit-life-and-effects.md` (**proposed**). Phase: 3 by date, ROADMAP Phase 5 by
kind (polish), pulled forward at Michael's request; no ROADMAP box, and no row here is on the
Phase 3 gate path (P88b → P90 → P94 → P93b). Rows P132-P154; the lead's acceptance row is P26aq.

## Goal
Michael, 2026-11-02 (translated): turrets jerk when they fire, soldiers move their legs, standing
units shift a little, dust behind tanks, smoke from the refinery, a turning radar, "the more small
details the better", and a superweapon launch that leaves its building and a strike that comes down
from the sky onto the chosen area. All of it view and art, deterministic in look (the same tick
looks the same), inside rule 6's budget, honest to the fog, in both orientations and on the web.

## Out of scope
Sound (Phase 5), camera shake (the camera moves only by the player's hand), shaders and Particles
nodes (ADR 0027 §3), any sim rule or state (one event kind only), life on last-seen ghosts, new HUD
feedback, death animations beyond today's burst and fade, a settings screen (Phase 5).

## The performance test
`tests/view/test_life_budget.gd` (P146) loads the perf arena scenario (150 mobile rows) under the
main scene's view layers plus `LifeFx`, runs 900 ticks in `SYNC` with four `_process` calls per
tick (alpha 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75) and pins: node count and `Performance.OBJECT_COUNT` flat after the first 60 ticks;
life sprite writes (the `RigLife`/`BuildingLayer` counters) at most 60 per frame on average and 150
in any one frame; ring slots in use never above capacity; each life layer's `_draw` draws from one
texture. Estimated frame cost at 150 units, from GDScript's 1 to 2 µs per touched row on the phone
(an estimate, judged by P147's card):

| Row | Work per frame | Writes per frame |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P136 recoil | one `bsearch` per `SHOT` (per tick); one offset per kicking sprite | about 15 in a full fight |
| P140 idle | one check per standing vehicle in view | at most 1 per vehicle per 80 ms, about 20 |
| P142 walk | one stride sum per moving squad, inside the existing rig loop | at most 1 per squad per 100 ms at 1 tile/s, about 10 |
| P144 anims | one frame index per animated building | about 2 (10 buildings, 4 to 12 frames/s) |
| P138 dust | one spawn test per moving vehicle in view; 96 quads at most | 0 (quads) |
| P145 smoke | one schedule test per emitter; quads shared in 96 slots | 0 (quads) |
| P141 strike | only while a strike plays; 8 slots | 0 (quads) |

Total about 0.3 ms per frame against ADR 0027 §9's 0.56 ms; draw calls 14 → 16 (+1 to 2 during a
strike).

## Proposed skill text (svg-art-style; on acceptance, with Michael's yes)
- Technical rules, the Naming line: "... `part` `hull`, `turret`, `head`, `shadow`, `walk`, `idle`
  or `anim`; a part with more than one frame per direction adds `_f<K>` (K from 0), and a building's
  `anim` part has no direction (`.../bld/refinery_anim_f0.svg`); ..."
- Drawing rules, a new bullet: "Life (ADR 0027): poses are model poses (`walk` 4 and `idle` 2 frames
  per squad direction, a building's `anim` at most 16 frames cropped to the moving element), never
  edited frames; shadows are not posed. Life effects (`svg/fx/life_atlas.svg`,
  `svg/fx/strike_atlas.svg`) carry no team colour: flames yellow-white (hue 46° or more), smoke and
  dust grey. The sorted world's cross-faction page stays at most 3584 px tall at bake scale 1.0
  (`tests/view/test_iso_atlas_budget.gd`)."
- Originality, appended to the list: "a beam or column from orbit, a missile rising from silo
  doors, a mushroom cloud".

## Risks
- **Atlas growth.** The estimate (about 3450 of 3584 px) leaves little room; P137's test fails the
  first art row past it, and the row cuts frames or crops rather than letting the bake scale drop.
- **Phone cost.** The per-row bounds are counts, not times; P147's card decides. Fallbacks in order:
  idle shake off, dust interval doubled, `reduced` as the default on the web preview.
- **Readability** (GDD pillar 1): motion everywhere can drown the signals. Amplitudes and rates are
  exports; each row's phone check judges them, and Michael can turn one off without a code change.
- **Art effort.** Pose groups for four squads and ten buildings are real model work (P139, P143);
  blockout-quality poses first, polish later.
- **`STRIKE` and event-count tests.** A sim test that counts a `SUPERWEAPON` tick's events changes
  in P133, in the same PR.
- **`main.tscn`.** One node, rebased on the HUD lanes; the lead's combined-tree check.
- **`UnitLayer.gd` length** (708 lines): every row keeps its logic in `RigLife.gd` and adds calls
  only.
