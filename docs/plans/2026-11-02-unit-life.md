## Goal
Michael, 2026-11-02 (translated): turrets jerk when they fire, soldiers move their legs, standing
units shift a little, dust behind tanks, smoke from the refinery, a turning radar, "the more small
details the better", and a superweapon launch that leaves its building and a strike that comes down
from the sky onto the chosen area. All of it view and art, deterministic in look (the same tick
looks the same), inside rule 6's budget, honest to the fog, in both orientations and on the web.

## Out of scope
Sound (Phase 5), camera shake (the camera moves only by the player's hand), shaders and Particles
nodes (ADR 0027 §3), any sim rule or state (one event kind only), life on last-seen ghosts, new HUD
feedback, death animations beyond today's burst and fade, a settings screen (Phase 5).

## Acceptance per row
Every row: its suite passes alone, gdformat and gdlint are clean, no `Time`, `randi`, `randf`,
`RandomNumberGenerator` or Particles node in a life file (`test_life_purity.gd`, P136), no allocation
on its frame path (the source scan), no node per frame, every number it uses read from
`LifeSettings` or a sheet; a phone-preview check line in the PR. Tests of `mix`-driven behaviour
assert exact counts for fixed id sets (mix is deterministic), never "about".

- **P133** A valid `SUPERWEAPON` writes exactly one `STRIKE` record with the building id, the
  clamped point, the kind, the owner and the landing tick (the rupture's `strike_tick`; 0 for a
  blackout); a dropped one (every drop reason of `apply_superweapon`) writes none; the golden and
  checkpoint hashes are unchanged (`test_golden_hashes.gd`, `test_replay_play.gd`), both
  implementations; Combat's header comment names the real writer order.
- **P134** A model with poses generates `<id>_<part>_d<NN>_f<K>.svg` (and `<id>_anim_f<K>.svg` for a
  building) deterministically, in a test's temporary output, never committed here; a group's offset
  and its turn about x, y or z through a pivot are pinned on a crafted model; every committed frame
  and sheet is byte-identical before and after the row (staleness test); `IsoSheetDef.frames` is 1
  for every part of today; `emitters`, `marks`, `stride_milli` and `anim_ms` round-trip.
- **P135** Both SVGs pass the hand-drawn rules (elements, 28 KB, no pattern or filter); every region
  of STYLE.md's "Life effects" table exists with clear space; no colour in either key band at
  saturation 0.30 or more; flames' hue at least 46°; the Life page shows each sprite on grass and
  sand at the three zooms.

  consumer falls back to `data/life_settings.tres`; `LifeClock.ms` is `(tick + alpha) x 1000 / 15`

  fails the first art row past it, and the row cuts frames or crops rather than letting the bake
  scale drop. P153 and P154 land only if the measured page leaves room.

  included). If the web preview's bake gets too long, its bake scale may start at 0.75 (ADR 0023 §2

  idle shake off, dust interval doubled, `reduced` as the default on the web preview, all of them
  `.tres` edits.

  is in `data/life_settings.tres`; each row's phone check judges them, and one effect can be turned
  down or off there without a code change.

  blockout-quality poses first, polish later.
- **`main.tscn`.** One node, rebased on the HUD lanes; the lead's combined-tree check.
