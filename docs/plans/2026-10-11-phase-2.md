Lessons from Phase 1 that shape this plan:
- Every PR ended at 390-440 changed lines once review fixes were in. The estimates below are
  first-commit sizes counted from each PR's listed contents (code after `gdformat`, which wraps
  every long list one name per line, plus 15-25 lines per test case and helpers). The rule:
  every PR estimated at 330 or more names a split line, and no estimate is above 400. If a first
  commit passes 400 lines, it is split along its named line before it opens, never after
  review. Declaration-heavy PRs (World v1) are counted after `gdformat`, not before.
- The plan PR (P26) is docs and is exempt from the ~400-line code rule, as the Phase 0 and
  Phase 1 plan PRs were. It carries this plan (about 1,660 lines), ADRs 0007-0011 (about 1,230)
  and the architect memory files.
- All PRs code against one contract table, including a column table for the World.
- File ownership is disjoint. A cross-owner change goes in a "Requests" section, except the few
  named one-line exceptions below, which would otherwise leave `main` red.
- At most two lanes run at once.
- Each PR names its phone check, or says none.
- Michael approves merges with "merge Px" (a batch such as "merge P27a-P29b" is fine, Phase 1
  answer 9).
- Tests pin every contract number literally, never derived from the same `.tres` (P1 review).
- Performance is measured from the first week (probe on the phone, harness in CI), not at the
  end.

## Goal
Every Phase 2 box in `docs/ROADMAP.md` is met on `main`:
- The sim runs at 15 Hz with World v1, Command v1, DetRandom, a state hash every second and a
  replay test that reproduces the final hash.
- Units path around rock and water with flow fields, and do not overlap (separation).
- Three unit types (light tank, rifle squad, rocket squad) fight with hitscan and projectiles.
  Damage depends on armour class, and dead units are removed.
- Fog of war (visible and explored) is drawn on the map and hides unseen enemies.
- The view interpolates units and projectiles at the display frame rate.
- `tests/perf/` runs 150 units for 900 ticks, prints ms per tick and fails above
  `tests/perf/budget.cfg` or above the deterministic cost bounds.

Then Michael runs the gate (see "Gate procedure"):
- the dev scene "Perf arena" shows `GATE: PASS` on the gate phone three runs in a row, with
  every unit on screen;
- its checkpoint hashes (APK and web) match the golden hashes that CI checks for the same
  commit.

Accepting ADRs 0007-0011 also makes the lead update `rts-sim-conventions` in P26 (list under
P26), because the skill must match the code agents write.

Out of scope (each needs a plan change first): buildings, production, economy, heavy tank,
artillery, harvester, splash damage, AI (including `PlayerView`, reserved by ADR 0010), minimap,
the "under attack" edge indicator and minimap ping (Phase 3 HUD, together with the minimap), a
gesture for moving without fighting (Phase 3 plan agenda), rig pooling (Phase 3, with
production), sound, info card, save and resume, writing replays to disk, wiring
`run-sim-smoke.sh` (Phase 4 box; it stays "no sim yet", and the determinism and perf suites stand
in for it in Phase 2).

## Interface contracts (all PRs code against these)
Every `class_name` script is PascalCase, everything else snake_case (ADR 0002 §2). "milli" means
milli-tiles, a "cell" is a tile index `(y / 1000) * map_cols + x / 1000`, and "row" is an index
into the per-unit arrays (ascending id). All sim classes `extends RefCounted` unless noted.
`local_player` in every view, input and UI contract below (`UnitLayer`, `FogLayer`,
`HealthBarLayer`, `ProjectileLayer`, `EffectsLayer`, `PickSnapshot`) means
`runner.scenario.local_player`, read the way Phase 1's `PlayerController._local_player()` reads
it.

    calls only at event cadence (line checks, `best_neighbour` into `nav_next`).

## Test plan (what is proven headless)
- **Numbers and data:** every contract number pinned literally, data validation over all unit
  files, `RulesDef` defaults equal the data (`test_defs`, `test_defs_data`).
- **Purity:** no float, time, Node, unseeded randomness, `hash(` or allocation inside loops in
  `game/sim` (`test_sim_purity`, with fixtures).
- **DetRandom:** the published reference vector, the transcribed seeding, `mul32` against the
  true product, stream independence, bounds, unbiased `next_int`, shuffle, state round-trip with
  high-bit words.
- **World:** tables, map grid, jitter and its draw count, hand-built worlds (type tables padded,
  the Phase 1-shaped world steps through every phase), removal without
  reordering over `ROW_ARRAYS`, events (stride 8), hash coverage of World and helper objects,
  every hashed scalar, `hash_hex`, `hash_parts`, pinned tick-0 hash.
- **Commands:** every drop rule per type, `MOVE`/`STOP`/`ATTACK`, order independence,
  encode/decode, probe neutrality.
- **Determinism and replays:** same seed → same hashes every 15 ticks (sequential and
  interleaved); different seed → different hashes; replay reproduces the final hash, finds a
  changed command at the next checkpoint and detects changed data; the runner at 30/60/120 fps
  and jittered frames equals the pure loop; three arena runs and golden hashes at 150 units over
  900 ticks; the same suites on arm64 (P51b).
- **Movement:** Phase 1 exactness on open maps, detours through gaps, per-class passability, no
  centre ever on a blocked tile, staggered line checks, line switching, derived references,
  double-buffered re-routing after a map change, stall, attack fields, time slicing equal to
  one-shot integration, cache sharing and eviction.
- **Separation:** the formula push, the fixed push for near-coincident pairs (units 1-3 milli
  apart separate), shares, clamps, neighbour caps, no push into rock, resting slots unmoved, the
  Phase 1 orders end without overlap, and every Phase 1 test that separation would change is
  listed (audited table) and changed in a named PR.
- **Probe:** fixed phase ids, marks in ascending id order, unmarked phases read 0, hashes
  unchanged by a probe.
- **Combat:** acquisition priority, staggering and stickiness, reach by order, `ATTACK` lock and
  fallback, chase then hold, no chase through rock; fire timing, hitscan with both positions,
  flight time, homing, misses on dead targets, all 4 armour classes, firing in the tick of
  death, overflow, the counter triangle.
- **Fog:** masks against brute force, overlaps, explored persistence, block indexing, staggered
  cadence, re-stamping only on tile change, dirty lists, initial stamp; visibility gates
  targeting and `ATTACK`.
- **View and input:** interpolation of units and projectiles, rig mapping by id across deaths
  and spawns with sync at every entry point, restart resets in every layer, turret aim, health
  bar density and shapes, the effects ring with a hitscan kill, fog texture states, dirty
  patching and update count, hidden and unpickable enemies; object-leak checks (no new Objects

  UI and scene test that these PRs could break is listed in the second audited table, with the

- **End to end:** tap to attack, death, selection and badge pruning, fog reveal and picking on

- **Performance:** deterministic cost bounds per tick, and min-of-3 mean and p95 against

## Open questions for Michael
1. **Gate phone.** The ROADMAP asks for 60 fps on a mid-range phone from 2021. Your phone
   (2424x1080, probably 2023-2024) runs game logic about 2.5 times faster than such a phone, by
   our estimate. Measured on this computer, the game's logic alone would fill 8-14 ms of a
   16.7 ms frame on a 2021 phone. That is too close to judge from a faster phone.
   **Recommendation:** buy a used 2021 mid-range Android phone (about 100 EUR, for example a
   Samsung Galaxy A52 or a Xiaomi Redmi Note 10 Pro), and let the gate run on it. Nothing waits
   for it: the plan is accepted on the probe numbers from your current phone, with the limits
   divided by an assumed slowdown of 2.5, and the work starts at once. When the old phone
   arrives, you run the probe on it once, and a small data-only change corrects the limits. If
   you prefer not to buy one, the gate runs on your phone with those stricter limits.
2. **Phase 2 content.** **Recommendation:** yes to all of these:
   - Three unit types: light tank, rifle squad, rocket squad. The heavy tank, artillery and
     harvester come with production in Phase 3.
   - The infantry is shown only in the perf arena (dev menu) until Phase 3 lets you build it.
   - On the normal test map the red tanks now fire back, are hidden by fog until your tanks see
     them, and rock and water appear.
   - Until Phase 3 there is no way to pull units out of a fight: every tap on the ground means

   This is the smallest set that proves armour classes, projectiles and pathfinding, and it keeps
