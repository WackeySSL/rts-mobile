## Goal
Michael's six points, after Basics on his phone (upright), for Basics and all three drills:
1. The help card stands in one place, at the top. It folds away and opens itself again at each new
   step.
2. A step counter is in one corner of the card.
3. The frame around the thing to tap blinks.
4. When a step needs several taps (BUILD, a tab, an item card), the highlight follows the current
   sub-target.
5. All of this holds for every lesson.
6. An animated hand shows the gesture: tap, double tap, long press, drag or pan, box and pinch.

Scope guard. Not in this plan:
- Voice or new sounds (the radio chirp stays as it is).
- New lessons or new Teach rows.
- Changes to the HUD layout itself (`HudCorners` is read, not changed).
- A camera that moves by itself.
- Translations.
- A tutorial for the How to play screen.

These go to the lead's follow-up list if a row asks for them.

## Command and data changes
- **Commands:** none. The guide sends no Command and never writes the sim. `STATE_FORMAT`
  unchanged.
- **Data (append only, old files load unchanged):**
  - `TutorialBeatDef`:
    - `lead_in: Array[TutorialLeadInDef]`.
    - `gesture: Gesture` (`AUTO, NONE, TAP, DOUBLE_TAP, LONG_PRESS, DRAG, BOX, PINCH_OUT,
      PINCH_IN`).
    - `gesture_vector_dp: Vector2i`.
  - `TutorialLeadInDef`: `anchor`, `anchor_control`, `anchor_tile`, `kind`, `value`, `type_id`,
    `gesture`.
  - `TutorialConditions.Kind` 18 `STRIKE_AIMING`.
  - `GuideSettingsDef` / `data/tutorial/guide_settings.tres`:
    - The card: `card_max_width_dp` 560, `card_wide_dp` 440, `card_max_height_pct` 45.
    - The blink: `blink_period_ms` 800, `blink_low_alpha_pct` 50 (25 first; raised in P591 for 3:1 on the HUD panel).
    - The hint: `hint_cycle_ms` 1600, `hint_rest_ms` 800, `hint_idle_ms` 2000, `hint_tap_ms` 180,
      `hint_drag_ms` 900, `hint_pinch_ms` 900, `hint_hold_extra_ms` 400, `hint_size_dp` 72.
  - Colours: `GuideTokens` (`ring`, `ring_stalled`, `hint_fill`, `hint_line`) and the
    `GuideCardThrough` panel in `theme.tres`.
- **Lesson content (P593):**
  - Four tab-only beats merge into the next beat as lead-ins: Basics `open-structures` and
    `infantry-tab`, Air `flight-tab`, Support `infantry-tab`. The counters become Basics 26,
    Strikes 10, Air 11 and Support 12.
  - Every drawer beat ends on the item card with a chain BUILD, then the tab, then the card.
  - Strikes `aim` gets the chain strike button, then a map point (`STRIKE_AIMING`).
  - Coverage ids and budgets are unchanged in sum. The enemies' `trigger_beat` ids (`groups`,
    `home`, `sell-last`) are kept.

## Test plan (what is proven headless)
- **Dock** (`test_guide_dock.gd`, pure):
  - At the six sizes of ADR 0015 §6 and on `web_phone_small` and `web_phone_portrait`, the dock is
    inside the safe rect.
  - It lies under the top row (the toast slot included, upright) and clear of every `HudCorners`
    part but the economy card.
  - It does not move when the full map opens in landscape, and it drops under the map upright.
  - It is clear of the side panel and its handle, and of the sheet.
  - It is mirrored in left-handed mode.
- **Card and tab** (`test_guide_tab.gd`, `test_coach_mark.gd`):
  - The card opens on every new beat, folds and unfolds by a pushed tap, and opens once on a stall.
  - A turn keeps the fold.
  - The counter reads 1/N at the first beat, N/N at the last, and the next number in a gap.
  - Skip shows on the first beat only (answer 4).
  - The tab, Next and Skip are at least 48 dp, and Skip and Next 12 dp apart.
- **Never hidden** (the sweep, `test_coach_mark_keeps_off_controls.gd`, rewritten):
  - It runs every beat and every lead-in step of every lesson file, at the six sizes and the two
    profiles. Each is tried in the state the beat before leaves, in its own condition's state and
    with the drawer closed.
  - The active target is the expected control or tile, visible and enabled, and a pushed touch at
    its centre reaches it, through the card where ADR 0048 §3 applies.
  - The prompt fits the height cap.
  - The card is at least 16 dp from an ad band.
  - See-through happens only for tile, world-follow and modal targets.
- **Blink** (`test_guide_blink.gd`):
  - The period and the low opacity come from the data, and the theme tokens resolve.
  - With reduced effects there is no Tween and the frame is steady.
  - A stalled beat doubles the ring.
- **Chain** (`test_tutorial_chain.gd`, `test_tutorial_director.gd`, `test_tutorial_conditions.gd`):
  - The furthest reached step wins, both ways (placing after the drawer closed, the drawer closed
    by hand).
  - `STRIKE_AIMING` works.
  - `target_changed` fires once per change.
  - `problems()` rejects tick kinds, empty names and missing anchors.
- **Hints** (`test_gesture_hint.gd`):
  - `pose()` is checked at fixed times for each kind.
  - The long press holds `long_press_ms` plus the extra from `data/input_settings.tres`.
  - With reduced effects the hint shows the static pose.
  - `MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE` is set, and the hint hides while a pushed touch is down and comes back
    after the idle time. No event is marked handled.
  - An off-screen tile gives a drag toward it.
- **Lesson data** (`test_lesson_data.gd`):
  - The drawer lint: a `Tile_*` or `Tab*` target has the drawer lead-ins.
  - Gestures fit their kinds.
  - The merged beats keep the union of the covers and the sum of the budgets.
  - The trigger ids are kept.
- **End to end:** `TutorialBot` taps exactly the control the ring is on for each lead-in
  (`mark.target_control()`), so a ring that does not follow fails the run. Every lesson still ends.
- **View only** (`test_guide_view_only.gd`): the bot's Basics with the guide on, then with reduced
  effects, gives the same command log and state hash.
- **Not headless:** whether the card reads well, the blink is noticed and the hand is understood.
  That is Michael's phone check (P596), upright and sideways, with Reduced effects on and off.

## Risks
1. **The card covers a world target on short landscape screens** (280 dp high). Answer 3 A keeps
   the card still and see-through, so the target stays visible and touchable, but the map behind
   is busier to read. P596's phone check judges it. If Michael then wants option B, it is a
   one-row change in P588 and needs his yes and a dated note in ADR 0048.
2. **Prompts too long for the cap.** The sweep fails at once, and P593 shortens the prompt. No
   scrolling text. Today's longest prompt is 149 characters.
3. **A chain that regresses** (placement closes the drawer). The rule "the furthest reached step
   wins" is tested both ways. A lead-in whose condition the next step undoes, written later, is
   caught by the sweep's regressed states (P595).
4. **Merged beats and the scripted enemy.** P543c/d's triggers name beat ids. P593 keeps `groups`,
   `home` and `sell-last`, and `test_lesson_data.gd`'s trigger test guards them.
5. **Hint confusion:** a fake finger moving while the player touches. The hint hides on any touch
   and for 2 s after, and it never takes input. Tested with pushed events.
6. **Flashing and accessibility.** The blink is 1.25 Hz, never fully off and small in area, under
   WCAG's three flashes a second. Reduced effects makes it steady. Stalled changes the shape as
   well as the colour.
7. **Serial lane T1** (five rows on `CoachMark.gd`). Splitting the file (a `GuideRing` and a
   `GuideCard` child) would allow parallel work but means a refactor row first. That was rejected
   as scope: each row is small and the reviews stay focused.
8. **Removed code with mutation history** (P543g's search). Its tests go with it in P588. The
   review checks that no kept case (Skip by a real touch, the ad band's 16 dp, the hidden-under-a-
   modal rule) is lost.
