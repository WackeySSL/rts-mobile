# How to play (onboarding source)

Living list of everything a player can do. Each player-facing feature adds or updates its entry
in the PR that ships it (CLAUDE.md, Workflow). Phase 5 builds the interactive tutorial and the
in-game "How to play" screen from this list (ROADMAP Phase 5), so a feature without an entry here
is a feature players will not learn.

**Teach** marks what a new player will not find alone: the tutorial must show it, not only the
help screen. Timings and sizes live in `data/`, so entries name the setting instead of a number.

## Camera
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| Drag one finger | Pans the map | |
| Pinch | Zooms about the fingers; on release it settles on the nearest of three zoom levels (`zoom_levels` in `data/input_settings.tres`) | |
| Look at the map | The map is drawn at an angle, as a diamond: every tile is a diamond and the map's corners point up, down, left and right. Drag, pinch and taps work as before, and a tap on the ground sends the units to the point under your finger | |
| Drag the map to its edge | The camera stops at the map's edge: the middle of the screen never leaves the map. The edge can reach the middle of the screen, so what lies under the BUILD button, the build drawer, the group column or the panels at the top can be scrolled into view; beyond the edge the screen is dark grey. Turning the phone keeps the middle of the screen where it was | |
| Look at the round radar at the top right | The ground round your view: a small round window, drawn at the same angle as the map, with a white frame for what the screen shows. Tap it and the whole map unfolds under it, as a small diamond like the map itself (on a very short screen it unfolds from the top instead, to the left of the radar and of the strike button above BUILD, if you have one; if you keep it open with its pin while you aim, it stands left of the strike button's check mark and X too, and gets a little narrower to leave the pill its room; on a very short screen where even that does not fit, a pinned map folds when you start to aim, and the radar opens it again after the aim). Ground you have never seen is black and ground you saw before is dimmed. Your buildings and units show in your colour, as small marks the size of what they stand on; the enemy's show only where you see them now, in its colour, and bigger and rounder, so you can tell them apart without colour: an enemy unit is a small cross, an enemy building a rounded blob. A building you saw and then lost sight of stays as a dimmed blob until you see that spot again. Tap or drag on the full map and the view jumps there: the middle of the screen goes to the ground point under your finger, and a drag keeps following it; a touch beside the diamond lands on the nearest edge of the map. With units selected, hold it to send them there (see the next row). It folds away by itself after a few seconds without a touch (`radar_close_ms` in `data/input_settings.tres`), unless you tap its pin (the thumbtack, left of the arrows button in the head of the map; it stays pinned until you tap it again, and for the rest of the match); the arrows button folds it at once. A touch on the battlefield (drag, pinch, tap, hold) folds an unpinned map while the touch does its work as always (a pinned map stays); a touch on the map itself never folds it, and a touch on a button folds it only when that button opens something else. The map and the build drawer are one at a time, sideways and upright: opening the drawer (BUILD, tapping your Headquarters, barracks or vehicle factory, or the drawer coming back after a placement you began in it) folds the map, pinned or not, and the pin stays; opening the map slides the drawer away. The radar is never offline | yes |
| Hold the full map with units selected | Keep a finger still on the full map for `long_press_ms` (`data/input_settings.tres`) and the selected units move to the ground point under it (a finger resting there keeps the map from folding): the same move a ground tap sends, with the chevrons where they are going and a short buzz. It is always a move: tankers held over an oil or gas field drive there but do not load (tap the field on the map for that), and after a few seconds they go back to the field they worked (`harvest_resume_ticks` in `data/rules.tres`). The view has jumped there already, so you see them go. One hold is one order, however long you keep resting; lift and hold again for another. A short tap, or a finger that slides (it moves the view instead), orders nothing, and neither does a hold with nothing or only a building selected, or while you place a building or aim a strike. A hold beside the diamond sends them to the nearest edge of the map, and the chevrons are there. Turning the phone, or the full map folding away, while your finger is down cancels the hold | yes |

## Turn the phone
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| Turn the phone, on a menu or during a match | The game plays in portrait and in landscape and follows the phone's sensor. A match goes on while you turn: nothing restarts and no order is given, the map keeps its centre and the size of what is on it, and the controls keep their size and move to the layout of the other orientation | |
| Turn the phone while a finger is on the screen (a drag, a box or a pinch) | That gesture is cancelled, never turned into a tap or an order: no unit is selected or sent. Lift the finger and start the gesture again | |
| Turn the phone with the full map or the build drawer open | Each keeps its state through the turn and moves to its place in the other orientation: the full map under the radar, the drawer from the right edge sideways or from the bottom upright, still on the tab you had. A drawer that was closed stays closed | |
| Auto-rotate is off in the phone's settings | The game stays in the orientation the phone is in. The game cannot force a turn and has no lock of its own | |

## Units
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| Look at your units | Each unit is drawn from the picture set of its side (the Derrick League or the Lumen Accord) in its player's team colour (your side's own: orange for the Derrick League, cyan for the Lumen Accord, and the other player wears the other one) with a shadow on the ground under it; units of two players on one side still differ by colour. The selection panel shows the unit's item card | |
| Watch a unit drive or fight | The picture turns in steps to face where the unit drives (infantry in coarser steps); a tank's turret turns on its own to face its target while the hull keeps its heading; infantry turn as a whole toward their target. A turret kicks back when it fires, only for a shot you can see. A vehicle rumbles a little while it drives and holds still when it stops; a standing tank with a turret and no target now and then turns its gun a notch to one side and back (`drive_shake_px`, `look_every_ms` in `data/life_settings.tres`; both are off with reduced effects). Infantry swing their legs as they walk, faster when they walk faster, and stop with the squad; a squad that stands still for a moment shifts its weight now and then (`idle_after_ms` and `idle_every_ms` in `data/life_settings.tres`). A vehicle under half its health is sooty; under a quarter it is scorched and a loose plate hangs off it. Infantry do not show it (`vehicle_stage_percent` in `data/life_settings.tres`). A damaged vehicle smokes; under a quarter of its health it burns and throws sparks (the sparks are off with reduced effects; the numbers are in `data/life_settings.tres`). A unit lower on the screen is drawn in front of one above it | |

## Buildings
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| Look at a building | Each building is drawn from the picture set of its side in its player's team colour, standing on its footprint with a soft shadow on the ground. It is drawn like a unit: a unit lower on the screen, in front of it, is drawn over it, and one higher up, behind it, is hidden by it. A defence's gun head turns on its own to face its target and stays turned while it has none. Some buildings have a moving part: the Signal Mast's antenna and the Relay Tower's array turn, the Wind Park's rotors and the Turbine Hall's fans spin, the Deep Bore's drill turns, sparks climb the Blackout Array, and a beacon turns on the Grid Hub and the Crew Hall; two of a kind do not move in step. The part stands still while the building is under construction or switched off, and yours move at half speed while you use more power than you make (`low_power_rate` in `data/life_settings.tres`). Finished, switched-on buildings give off smoke, steam or flames from their stacks and vents (the Derrick League's Rigworks and Turbine Hall smoke, its Cracking Plant gives steam from its column and burns a flame at its flare stack, and the Lumen Accord's Synthesis Works gives steam from its vent drums); you see an enemy building's only while you see the building | |
| Look at the enemy's buildings | An enemy building is drawn only while you see the ground it stands on now; one cell of its footprint is enough, and then the whole building shows. Out of sight it is not drawn as it is, but see the next row | |
| Watch a damaged building burn | A damaged building burns at one spot with thin grey smoke; under a third of its health it burns from inside with thick black smoke and throws sparks. You see an enemy building's smoke and fire only while you see the building. The sparks are off with reduced effects (`damage_*` in `data/life_settings.tres`) | |
| Look at the fog | An enemy building you have seen stays on the map as a dimmed picture, in its side's colours, where you last saw it, even if it has been destroyed since out of your sight. Seeing any one tile of the building is enough, its edge or corner as well as its middle. The picture stays until you see any tile of that spot again: if the building is there, it shows as it is; if not, the picture goes. A building destroyed while you see any tile of it leaves no picture. The picture is hidden while you see any tile of the building, and comes back when you look away. The dimmed picture has no health bar and cannot be tapped (a tap there is a tap on the ground), and your units are drawn in front of it | |
| A building is hit, or selected | A bar shows above it, as wide as its footprint (small, medium or large), filled by the hp it has left, for a few seconds after each hit and for as long as the building is selected. Your bars are plain, an enemy's has a notch in the top edge. An enemy building the fog hides shows none | |
| A building is destroyed | It disappears from the map and the units behind it are no longer hidden. One you saw fall, and any of your own, leaves a crater on the ground where it stood, as wide as its footprint, for the rest of the match | |

## Selection
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| Tap an own unit | Selects that unit only. Tap anywhere on its picture, the middle of it included, not only on the ground under its feet: a unit is hit round the middle of its picture, and where two are close the one whose middle is nearer your finger is picked. A unit is easy to hit: at least 48 dp across (`hit_radius_dp` in `data/input_settings.tres`), however small it is drawn. Selected units show a white oval ring lying on the ground under them, as big as the unit's circle on the ground and never smaller than 36 dp across | |
| Tap your own building | Selects that building only, and a unit standing at the same spot is picked instead of it. Tap anywhere on the building's picture, the part that stands up over the ground included, not only on the ground it covers; where two buildings overlap, the one drawn in front is selected. A building is easy to hit: at least 48 dp across (`hit_radius_dp` in `data/input_settings.tres`), however small it is drawn. It gets a frame of four corner brackets and its health bar, the panel names it, and a Headquarters, barracks or vehicle factory opens the tab it builds | |
| Tap an enemy building you can see, with nothing selected | Selects it for its information only: an amber frame and its notched health bar show, and the panel names it. It takes no orders, and an enemy building the fog hides cannot be tapped | |
| Tap the ground with any building selected except a barracks or a vehicle factory | Lets go of the building (also an enemy building you looked at). Nothing is ordered and nothing moves, and a tap on an enemy unit counts as a tap on the ground. A barracks or a vehicle factory is different: the same tap sets its rally flag (see Orders) and it stays selected | yes |
| Double-tap an own unit | Selects every own unit of that type on screen. A building never joins a double-tap, a box or "All army" | yes |
| Long-press, then drag | Draws a box while the finger moves; on release it selects the own units inside (`long_press_ms` in `data/input_settings.tres`). A box with a unit that fights among tankers leaves the tankers out; a box with only tankers in it selects them | yes |
| Long-press and drag a box with no own unit inside | Clears the selection, a selected barracks or vehicle factory included | yes |
| Any selection | The panel at the bottom centre; in portrait a line at the top, from the left edge and under the pill's line (the Dev and Surrender buttons and the pill stand above it). It shows each selected unit type or building with its icon, its display name (a very long name is cut short with an ellipsis) and its count, and a total for mixed types; it hides when nothing is selected. Tapping it does nothing. In portrait the line never grows past the screen: when the types do not fit it drops the names, then the total, and then shows the first types with a "+N" for the types it leaves out | |
| A selected unit or building is destroyed | It leaves the selection, and the count in the panel drops (a building's frame goes with it) | |

## Orders
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| With units selected, tap the ground | Move: the units drive there without fighting, also out of a fight; on arrival they fire at enemies within weapon range without moving; chevrons lying flat on the ground close in on the point | yes |
| With units selected, tap an enemy | Attack: the units drive to weapon range of that enemy, stop there and fire at it until it is destroyed, then attack-move on to where it stood; a flat diamond on the ground marks it. An enemy building is attacked the same way once the middle of it is in your sight: a tap anywhere on its picture counts, also where one of your own buildings is drawn in front of it. One you see only at its edge, or the fog hides, is a tap on the ground | yes |
| Select a barracks or a vehicle factory, then tap the ground | A rally flag stands there at once, in your colour (a small pennant over a ring for infantry, a wide banner over a square frame for vehicles), the phone gives a light buzz (`haptic_ms` in `data/input_settings.tres`, if haptics are on) and a line runs from the selected building to it. A tap off the edge of the map puts the flag on the edge. The building stays selected, so another tap moves the flag. Units built from then on drive to the flag; one flag serves every barracks, the other every vehicle factory; units already built stay where they are, and tankers ignore it. The flag stays on the map when you let go of the building | yes |
| With only tankers selected, tap an oil or gas field | The tankers drive there and load, with the same chevrons as a move. A tap within a fingertip of the field counts (`hit_radius_dp` in `data/input_settings.tres`). A tap on bare ground moves them there instead, and a tanker you sent away goes back to work by itself after a few seconds (`harvest_resume_ticks` in `data/rules.tres`). The tap is an order to load only when oil or gas is left within `harvest_pick_milli` of it (`data/rules.tres`); on a field that is dry, or where the oil is further than that, the tankers move there instead | |

## Combat
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| Enemies come within weapon range of your standing units | The units fire at them on their own, without an order, until they are destroyed or out of range; units on a move order do not fire until they have arrived; a destroyed unit disappears from the map | |
| Units that finish an attack meet more enemies | They stop to fight: they drive to weapon range of the enemy they picked, fire until it is destroyed, pick the next one, and go on to where the enemy you tapped stood when no enemy is left within their reach | yes |
| A unit is selected, or has just been damaged | A health bar shows above its picture, filled by the hp it has left (a unit that lives always shows a little fill). Selected units keep their bar; a damaged unit shows it for a few seconds; an enemy hidden by the fog shows none. Your bars are plain, enemy bars have a notch in the top edge, so you can tell them apart without colour | |
| A unit or building of yours is hit out of view (or killed by a hit out of view) | A red message at the top says what ("UNITS UNDER ATTACK", or the building's name), with "Tap to jump there": tap it and the view jumps to the hit (an open build drawer folds). A red wedge on the rim of the round radar points that way (a red ring on the radar instead when the hit is inside it), and the full map pings the spot. One hit near the last one soon after raises nothing new (`attack_cue_area_tiles`, `attack_cue_ms`); the message and the wedge go after `attack_toast_ms` (all in `data/input_settings.tres`). While the full map is open you get only the ping | yes |

## Terrain
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| Look at the ground | Every tile is a diamond of grass, sand, scrub, rock, water or dark oil-stained earth, drawn in two slightly different arrangements so that a stretch of one kind does not look stamped; outside the map's edge the screen is dark grey | |
| Send units across rock, water or scrub | Rock and water block all units, and scrub (the patterned ground) blocks vehicles but not infantry, which walks across it; a unit that cannot cross drives around to the point you tapped | yes |

## Economy
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| Look at an oil field | A field is a patch of dark, oil-stained ground with a wellhead standing in it. A full field is one black pool; as oil is taken the pool shrinks to a small one with bare earth round it (half) and then to a cracked stain (dry), and the field fills up again slowly, a step at a time | yes |

## Fog
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| Look at the map | Black ground has never been seen. Dimmed ground was seen before, but enemy units there are not shown, and an enemy building there shows only as the dimmed picture of what you last saw, when you saw any tile of it (see Buildings). Ground that one of your units sees now is clear, and the enemies on it show, with their shots and blasts. Driving out lights new ground and leaves a dimmed trail behind; your own units always show | yes |
| With units selected, tap near an enemy the fog hides | It counts as a tap on the ground: the units move to the point you tapped, with chevrons, and the hidden enemy is not attacked. Once one of your units sees it, the same tap attacks it | |
| Fight in the fog | Your units only attack enemies your side can see, whichever of your units sees them. An enemy that slips out of sight is no longer attacked: units you sent against it attack-move on to where it was last seen, and the others look for a new target | |

## Groups (left edge)
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| Tap "ALL" at the top of the column on the left edge | Selects every own unit that can fight, anywhere on the map. Tankers are left out, and so is every building | |
| Tap "+" with units selected | Saves them as the next free group (1-3); the phone buzzes and the group appears in the column with its number and count. "+" is gone while no unit is selected or all three groups hold units | yes |
| Tap a group | Selects the units stored in that group | |
| Hold a group (`group_assign_ms` in `data/input_settings.tres`) | Overwrites it with the current selection; the phone buzzes and the count shows. With no unit selected a hold does nothing, so a resting finger never empties a group | yes |
| A grouped unit is destroyed | It leaves the group; the group's count shows the new number, and a group with no unit left leaves the column | |
| Look at the group column | It stands on the left edge, sideways under the Dev and Surrender buttons, upright above the bottom edge and left of BUILD; more groups than fit in a column make a second column to the right. While you aim a strike, upright, it shows three buttons in one column and the rest scroll, so it never sits against the check mark and the X; drag the column to reach the rest (a drag selects nothing). Upright, the open build drawer's sheet covers the column | |

## The screen in battle
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| Look at the screen | Your credits and your power are in a pill at the top left, after the Dev button (after the Surrender button where Dev is hidden), sideways and upright alike; while the build drawer is open the pill folds away and the drawer's top shows them instead (the coin and your credits with "+N/min" under them, your income per minute, and the power bar with its numbers where they fit; it turns red with the warning triangle and "LOW POWER" there too), so they are on screen once. The economy card opens only from the pill, not from the drawer's top. The pill shows the coin and your credits, and a bolt and a power bar that fills with the power your buildings use against the power they make, with both numbers inside it: the pill grows to make room for them as far as the screen allows (never into the top-right corner), and on a narrow screen they are left out and the bar and the bolt say it alone. The digits keep their places, so the numbers do not jitter as they change | yes |
| Use more power than you make | The pill gets a red edge, the bar turns red and full, the bolt becomes a warning triangle and the word "LOW POWER" takes the place of the numbers in the bar, and the pill's edge and plate and the triangle pulse. Every building of yours that uses power gets an orange warning sign above it, and every defence gets the "switched off" sign instead (not a building that is still being built or is switched off by a blackout): what you queue is built slower (`low_power_percent` in `data/rules.tres`) and your defences hold their fire until you make enough power again | yes |
| Tap the credits or the power in the pill | A card opens under the pill and the game goes on behind it. It shows your credits with your income per minute (what your tankers brought in over the last minute; in the first minute, what they brought in so far), how many tankers you have, the power you use and make, and a chip for each kind of building that uses power with the power its buildings use together (a building that is switched off does not count). On low power it also says that production is slower and your defences are offline. The next touch closes it, on the map (which then does its own work, a move or a selection), on the card or on a control (which then does its own work). A tap on the numbers while the card shows only closes it: tap the numbers again to open it | |
| Spend or earn credits | When your credits fall (you queue something) a red "-N" shows under the credits for a moment (`economy_flash_ms` in `data/input_settings.tres`) and the number counts down to the new value; when they rise (a tanker delivers, a cancelled item is paid back) a green "+N" shows and the number is the new one at once. Changes inside that moment add up in one chip, which says the sign in text too, so it is not colour alone. Nothing flashes when a match starts | |

## Build menu (the build drawer)
| Action | Result | Teach |
|---|---|---|
| Look at the screen | The map fills it: the pill at the top left, the round radar at the top right (see Camera) and the round BUILD button at the bottom right, sideways and upright alike, with the Dev and Surrender buttons beside the pill and the group column on the left edge (the order bar comes later). A match starts with the build drawer closed. BUILD shows a ring that fills with the item that finishes first among everything you have queued, and a number for how many items you have queued in all (no number when nothing is queued) | yes |
| Tap BUILD | The build drawer slides in over the map: from the right edge sideways, as a panel at least a third of the screen wide, and from the bottom upright, as a sheet over the lower half; the game goes on behind it. It shows three tabs with their names and a grid of cards (at least two across; the grid scrolls up and down when it is longer than the room). BUILD hides while the drawer is up. Your credits and power move into its top (the pill folds away until it closes) | yes |
| Close the drawer | Tap the arrow at the top of the drawer, touch the map (drag, pinch, tap or hold: it slides away and your touch does what it always does), or swipe it back toward the edge it came from (sideways a drag to the right on the panel, upright a drag down from its top bar; a drag up and down on the cards scrolls them instead). Touching a tab or a card never closes it. Starting a placement (see below) or aiming a strike closes it too (`menu_slide_ms` and `drawer_swipe_dp` in `data/input_settings.tres`) | yes |
| A grey ring with "!" on BUILD, or a "Ready" chip beside it | The grey ring and the "!" disc at BUILD's top left mean that nothing you queued is moving (the factory is gone or switched off, or you have as many units as you may); the ring stops where it was and goes back to its colour when the queue moves again. Low power only slows the ring, it does not turn it grey. A "Ready" chip with the building's picture stands left of BUILD while a building is finished and waiting; tap it and the drawer opens on Structures with that building's card in view (tap the card to place it). The chip goes when the building is placed (and, in a later update, with the "Ready" state itself) | |
| Tap a tab | It opens: Structures, Infantry or Vehicles, each an icon over its name; the open tab is the lighter one with a thick bar under it | |
| Tap an item in the open tab | One of it is queued and paid for at once; the icon fills while it is built, and a number on it counts how many of it are queued. Each card shows the item's name under its cost (a long name is cut short with an ellipsis). A padlock on a dimmed icon means a building you need is missing (for the Derrick League: a Turbine Hall for a Bunkhouse, a Bunkhouse for infantry, a Signal Mast for the Mortar Rig). Stripes mean it cannot be queued now: you cannot pay it (its cost turns amber) or its queue is full (`queue_slots` in `data/rules.tres`). A finished building shows "Ready" where its price was (tap it to place it, see the next rows). A tap on a padlocked or striped item shakes it and does nothing. The grid scrolls up and down with a drag, and a drag never queues anything | yes |
| Hold an item (`long_press_ms` in `data/input_settings.tres`) | The last one of it you queued is cancelled and its cost is paid back in full; the phone buzzes. An item with none queued does nothing, however long you hold | yes |
| Tap your Headquarters, barracks or vehicle factory (the Derrick League's Rigworks, Bunkhouse or Heavy Yard) | The building is selected (see Selection) and the build drawer comes up, if it was down, on the tab it builds: Structures, Infantry or Vehicles. Tap it again after you opened another tab and the drawer comes back to its tab | |
| Open any tab | It lists only the buildings and units of your own side, and those that both sides share; the other side's items are never shown | |
| Tap BUILD while you place a building | It works during placement: the ghost stays and the cards are back (the drawer slides away again with the next touch of the map, or when you tap a "Ready" card). The drawer also comes up on its own when you tap your Headquarters, barracks or vehicle factory (on the tab it builds), and comes back when you finish or cancel a placement you began from a card in it | yes |
| Reach a finished building | Open the drawer with BUILD and the Structures tab: the finished building shows "Ready" where its price was; tap that card to place it. (Or tap the "Ready" chip beside BUILD, see the row about the grey ring and the chip, above) | |
| Tap a finished building ("Ready" on its card) | Placement begins: a ghost of the building appears in the middle of the view, its footprint marked as diamonds on the ground with the building faded over them, and a check mark and an X appear beside the ghost, on its right (on its left near the right edge of the screen, or where a panel of the HUD is in the way, as on a short sideways phone; above it when neither side has room); the build drawer slides away, so the map has the room. They hide while you drag the ghost and come back when you let go. A green wash and a solid frame mean it fits. A red wash with stripes and a small sign mean it does not, and the sign says why: it leaves the map, the ground is rock, water or built on, a unit stands there, you have not explored the ground, it is too far from your buildings (`build_reach_tiles` in `data/rules.tres`) or you have as many buildings as you may (`building_cap`). Tap the check mark to build it where the ghost stands (it waits dimmed while the ghost does not fit, see the next rows), or the X to keep it for later: the finished building stays "Ready" on its card, and the build drawer comes back up if it was up when you began. Lifting a finger never builds anything. If you bring the drawer up with BUILD while you place (it works during placement, and the ghost stays), the cards are back: tap the "Ready" card again and the drawer slides away and the ghost jumps back to the middle of the view (nothing happens while the building order is on its way); hold the card and the last one of that building you queued is cancelled and paid back; if that was the finished one, placement ends. If you were aiming a strike, the aim ends and nothing is fired. If you aim a strike while the ghost shows, placement ends and the drawer stays down | yes |
| Tap the check mark | The building order is sent once and the check mark goes dim, so a second tap sends nothing. The ghost stays where it stands, held, until the game answers, a fraction of a second later: the building appears, placement ends and the build drawer comes back up if it was up when you began. The X still works meanwhile, but the order is already on its way. While the game is paused (or the app is in the background) the order waits for the game to run again; when the match ends, placement ends | |
| The ghost turns red with the word "Blocked" after the check mark | The game refused the order, for instance because a unit moved onto the ground after the ghost was last checked. The ghost is red with stripes and a sign and the word "Blocked" stands under it; the check mark stays dim and the finished building stays "Ready". Move the ghost (drag it, or tap the ground) and tap the check mark again, or tap the X. If the finished building is gone by then (an earlier order, with the X and a second tap on its card, already built it), placement just ends | |
| While the ghost shows, drag from the ghost or from just beside it (`ghost_grab_dp` in `data/input_settings.tres`) | The ghost follows your finger and sits above it (`ghost_lift_dp`), so the finger never hides it, and it snaps to tiles. A drag that starts anywhere else pans the map as usual. Tap the ground and the ghost jumps to that tile; a tap on the ghost itself leaves it where it is. The check mark and the X stand just outside that margin, so a press in the margin does not land on them (only in a tight corner, where they have no other room, may they overlap it: pan the ghost out of it). Pinch and two-finger drag work as ever.
