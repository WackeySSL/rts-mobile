# SVG art style

Look (ADR 0023, Michael 2026-10-26): the canvas's Version B look without its geography, readable
at small size on a phone. Clean flat-shaded vector, three tones per face, real materials and
believable proportions, small team markings; not cartoonish, not photoreal. Everything on the map
is drawn in the isometric 2:1 view. Units, turrets and buildings are simple 3D models in
`assets/art/models/` that the generator (`tools/artgen/`, `bash scripts/gen-art.sh`) turns into
SVG frames (P101-P104 modelled all twelve units and sixteen buildings); ADR 0023 §5 still lets a
building be hand-drawn in the projection or get a hand-drawn detail overlay. Ground diamonds,
fields, icons, glyphs, UI parts and effects are hand-written SVG.
Two factions: the Derrick League (oil and gas: poured concrete, riveted steel, rust, olive drab
vehicles, gas spheres, flare stacks, pumpjacks, pipe racks, warning stripes, warm lamps; wedges and
trapezoids) and the Lumen Accord (white ceramic, graphite, glass, blue-grey vehicles with dark
patches, wind turbines, solar panels, battery banks, mint status light; hexagons and rounded
panels). `assets/art/STYLE.md` holds the full rules. P101 and P103 deleted the top-down unit files
and P91b's oblique buildings; the hand-drawn `svg/icons/item_<id>.svg` (top-down units, oblique
buildings) stay while `BuildSidebar` reads them. New art follows this file.

## Camera and scale
- Projection (ADR 0023 §1): a ground point (gx, gy) in tiles, z px above it, is drawn at
  `((gx - gy) x 32, (gx + gy) x 16 - z)` px at zoom 1. A tile is a 64 x 32 diamond. Never rotated.
- Author at 2x: 1 tile = 128 x 64 px in the SVG; the atlas bake rasterises at `bake_scale`.
- One light from the north-west for everything (Spec §02).
- Directions (Spec §04): vehicles, turrets and defence heads 16 (`d00` to `d15`, 22.5° steps),
  infantry squads 8 (the even ones). `d_k` faces the grid angle `(k - 6) x 22.5°` from +gx towards
  +gy: `d06` is south-east on screen (the Derrick League's default view), `d10` south-west (the
  Lumen Accord's), `d00` up the screen, `d08` down it.
- Anchors (Spec §03): a unit's frame is anchored at its ground point; a building's art origin is
  its footprint's back corner, 16 x n px (at 1x) above the centre of an n x n footprint. Every
  footprint is square.
- Canvases at 2x (starting values; the generator's sheets fix them): turreted vehicle hull 128x128,
  turret 96x96, shadow 128x64; infantry squad 96x64, shadow 96x48 (three figures per frame); 3x3
  building about 384x492, 2x2 about 256x328 (up to 150 px high at 1x); 1x1 defence heads 64x64.
- A rig's turret mount per hull direction, `bar_lift_px`, `pick_lift_px` and `muzzle_lift_px`
  come from the art id's `IsoSheetDef`, which the generator writes.

## Palette (hex)
- Derrick League (starting values; STYLE.md owns them): gun steel #6B7366, concrete #9F9A8E,
  weathered rust #6E5D50, dark iron #3B3F39, lamp warm white #FFF1D6, olive #7D8562.
- Lumen Accord: ceramic #D5DBDC, graphite #414A51, titanium #8A9398, glass #B4C8CF, blue-grey
  #7D8B96, status light mint #A8F5B0.
- Team zones are painted only in the faction's **key triad** (light, mid, dark): Derrick League
  #FF9446 #E8681F #B04B12, Lumen Accord #62D6EE #1FA6C8 #137C99. At match start the bake replaces
  those six exact hex strings with each player's triad (eight triads: Signal, Arktisk, Karmin,
  Kobolt, Guld, Magenta, Sne, Kul; player 1 Signal, player 2 Arktisk by default).
- **Key band** (a `tests/art` check): no other colour in a faction's frames may have an HSV hue
  within 20° of one of its key colours and a saturation of at least 0.30. Greys and muted
  materials (saturation below 0.30) are fine at any hue.
- Terrain: grass #5E7D4A / #6F8F57, dirt #8A6F4D, rock #6E6A66, water #2E5E7E / #3C7396: the base
  colours that `MapView` and the gallery keep (P52's cross-file facts); the iso ground diamonds and
  the oil and gas field stages follow STYLE.md's "Fog and terrain" and "Fields".
- UI accents (UI only, never in faction art): warning #F5A524, danger #E5484D, ok #30A46C.

## Drawing rules
- Faces are flat-shaded from the one light (Spec §02): top lightest, left side middle, right side
  darkest, a thin light edge on top edges. Edges are a dark shade of their own material, not one
  outline colour.
- Cast shadows are separate frames: every volume projected onto the ground away from the light,
  black at 35 % opacity as one group, about 0.8 x the height long. No shadow inside a hull or
  turret frame.
- The silhouette must be identifiable at 32 px in greyscale. No detail thinner than 2 px at 2x.
- Team colour covers about 8-12 % of the visible surface as bands, cranes, stripes and light
  strips, never large team plates.

## Technical rules
- One drawing per file with `width`, `height` and `viewBox="0 0 W H"` set to the canvas.
- Generated frames use only polygons and `g` with group opacity, with fixed decimals and a fixed
  order, so two runs give identical bytes; a `tests/art` case regenerates every model in memory and
  fails on any difference from the committed files. Edit the model and regenerate, never the frame.
- Hand-drawn files may use: path, rect, circle, ellipse, polygon, polyline, line, g, defs,
  linearGradient, radialGradient, clipPath, and use referencing only `href="#id"` in the same
  file; group opacity and dash arrays. A mask only with solid fills (Godot 4.7.2's ThorVG hides
  everything under a gradient-filled mask).
- Not allowed: pattern (ThorVG in 4.7.2 renders none), text, image, filter, foreignObject,
  external href, style blocks, scripts.
- At most 28 KB per file; integer or one-decimal coordinates in hand-drawn files.
- Naming (ADR 0023 §5): `assets/art/iso/<faction>/<category>/<type_id>_<part>_d<NN>.svg`, with
  `faction` `fossil` or `renew`, `category` `veh`, `inf`, `bld` or `fx`, `part` `hull`, `turret`,
  `head` or `shadow`; a building's body and shadow carry no direction (`.../bld/refinery_hull.svg`).
  `assets/art/iso/index.tres` maps each `type_id` to its sheet. Models:
  `assets/art/models/<faction>/<type_id>.tres`.
- Import: iso frames use the "Keep File" importer (the bake reads the SVG text; commit the
  `.import` files). Ground diamonds, field stages, props, the selection ring and the UI and item
  icons (the generated build cards included) stay normal SVG imports (scale 0.5 for 2x art):
  `assets/art/svg/ground/<terrain_id>_a.svg` and `_b.svg`, `assets/art/svg/fields/`,
  `assets/art/svg/icons/`, `assets/art/svg/ui/`.

## Originality
Describe each silhouette in words before drawing or modelling. No recognizable designs from other
games, for example twin-barrel super-heavy tanks (single barrels only), tesla-coil or prism
towers, airships, a dome on a radar tower, a mobile base that unfolds into a headquarters, oil
derricks to capture, engineers, flamethrower infantry, freeze or cryo weapons, or blue EMP
lightning. No geography (decision 1 of ADR 0023): no national coding, no flags, stars, wreaths,
crests or real insignia, no copied real weapons. When in doubt, choose a plainer real-world form.

## Gallery
Every asset appears in `scenes/dev/art_gallery.tscn` at gameplay zoom on grass and sand, in both
team colours, so consistency can be judged on a phone through the web preview.
